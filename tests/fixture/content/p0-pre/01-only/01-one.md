## One

### Statement

**Definition.** An affine variety is the zero set of a prime ideal of $k[x_1, \dots, x_n]$.

Lean: not in Lean.

**Proposition.** A point is an affine variety.

Lean: not in Lean.

### Examples

The origin of $\mathbb{A}^2$.
