Chapters 3 to 8 of P0 start the way. They build the varieties, the maps between them and the first examples. Chapter 5 gives the blow-up and the quadratic transformation of $\mathbb{P}^2$, the first birational maps. Chapter 8 gives the 27 lines on a cubic surface. They are $(-1)$-curves, and the cubic surface is the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at six points.
