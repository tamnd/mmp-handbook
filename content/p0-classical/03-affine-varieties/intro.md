# Affine varieties

This chapter studies the zero sets of polynomials in affine space. Throughout, $k$ is an algebraically closed field of any characteristic. The chapter assumes the algebra of a first course: rings, ideals, quotients, Noetherian rings and the Hilbert basis theorem. It cites two facts of P1 Chapter 4, Noether normalization and the behaviour of fields under integral extensions, and it proves everything else.

The main result is Hilbert's Nullstellensatz. It turns geometry into algebra: algebraic sets correspond to radical ideals, irreducible sets to prime ideals and points to maximal ideals. The last section uses it to show that affine varieties and their morphisms are the same thing as finitely generated reduced $k$-algebras and their homomorphisms, with the arrows reversed. P3 rebuilds this dictionary for schemes, where the field need not be algebraically closed and nilpotents are allowed.
