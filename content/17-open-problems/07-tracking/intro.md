# How the project tracks these problems

Chapters 1 to 6 give the open problems one at a time. This chapter gives the machinery that keeps their state. The project keeps the state in records, and the handbook copies the records. A later report gets a new state from the same records.

Section 7.1 gives the status table by dimension that spec 11.6 asks for, with the literature state and the Lean status of each cell, and the list of the open and claimed statements of the map. Section 7.2 gives the claim records C1 to C17. Section 7.3 gives the audit of AxiomMath/MMP and the trust questions Q0 to Q10. Section 7.4 gives the routes, their weights, the milestones, the lab and the gaps of Lean that block the statements.

All numbers in this chapter are those of the records on 2026-11-26. The map has 60 statements, S1 to S60. The library mmp-lean states 22 of them as theorems and 8 as open conjectures. It has the class claimed for 6 more. 24 have no Lean statement.
