# Minimal models, non-vanishing and abundance

When the program for a pair with $K + \Delta$ pseudo-effective ends, it ends with a minimal model: a model on which $K + \Delta$ is nef. Three conjectures say what happens there. The minimal model conjecture says that such a model exists. Non-vanishing says that some multiple of $K + \Delta$ has a section. Abundance says that $K + \Delta$ on a minimal model is semiample, so that it gives a morphism to the canonical model.

Section 2.1 treats minimal models and non-vanishing. Section 2.2 treats abundance and the conditional results of 2025 and 2026. Section 2.3 treats three related conjectures: generalized abundance, the Iitaka conjecture $C_{n,m}$ and the b-semiampleness of the moduli part, with the claim that finite generation fails for generalized pairs.

Spec 03.11 gives the shape of this part of the program: abundance has one main entrance, non-vanishing. Every conditional abundance result in the records goes through it. Route R5 of the project (weight 0.10) works on this cluster, and route R1 checks parts of the claims.
