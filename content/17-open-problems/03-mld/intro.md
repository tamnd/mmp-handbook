# ACC and LSC for minimal log discrepancies

The minimal log discrepancy $\operatorname{mld}(x; X, \Delta)$ of a pair at a point measures how far the pair is from being singular there. Two conjectures about this one number would give termination of flips in every dimension, by Shokurov's reduction: the ascending chain condition (ACC) and the lower semicontinuity (LSC). A third conjecture, the mld bound, says that the number is at most the dimension.

Section 3.1 treats the ACC. Section 3.2 treats the LSC and the mld bound, with the withdrawn claim of 2026. Section 3.3 treats the sharper reduction of Han and Liu and its toric case, which is Target T of the project.

Route R4 of the project (weight 0.10) attacks this cluster. Route R3 (weight 0.15) gives the toric cases, and experiment E2 of the lab gives the first data.
