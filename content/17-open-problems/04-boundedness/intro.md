# Boundedness and effective results

Birkar proved the boundedness of Fano varieties (BAB) and of complements. The open problems of this chapter come after BAB. They ask for bounds on Calabi-Yau varieties, on indices, on volumes and on the multiples of a divisor that are free or effective.

Section 4.1 treats boundedness after BAB: the index conjecture, the cone conjecture, the boundedness of Calabi-Yau 3-folds and the volume claims of Bie, Du and Yu. Section 4.2 treats the effective results: Fujita freeness and the effective non-vanishing conjecture, which a claim of 2026 refutes in dimension 4. Section 4.3 treats K-stability and the Cremona groups, where the map has no node yet.

Route R6 of the project (weight 0.10) attacks this cluster. Spec 04.7 says that many of its targets reduce to a finite search, a lattice computation or a numerical inequality. Route R1 checks the claims, and the lab experiments E1, E3, E4, E5 and E6 give the data.
