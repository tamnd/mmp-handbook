# Open problems

This volume is a dated report. Its date is 2026-11-26. It is the first release of Volume 17, the one that spec 11.10 of the project ties to milestone S1. Each later milestone gives a new dated report, and each report replaces the one before it. A reader who finds this text after 2026-11-26 must check the records of the project for changes. A claim of this report can be withdrawn, refereed or refuted after the date.

The volume lists the open problems of the minimal model program and says what the project knows about each one on the date. All content comes from the records of the project tamnd/mmp: the blueprint of nodes, the claim records in `claims/`, the audit record of AxiomMath/MMP, the Lean records of the library mmp-lean, and specs 01, 03, 04 and 06. Where the records give no answer, the text says "the records of the project do not say". The volume does not fill such a gap from memory.

The words of the volume have exact meanings.

- *Theorem* means a proof that referees accepted, as the records give it.
- *Open* and *conjecture* mean that no proof is known or claimed in a form that the records accept.
- *Claimed* means that a preprint, an AI manuscript or a Lean repository gives a proof, and that no referee and no reader of the project has checked it yet. A claimed result is never called proved in this volume, and no argument in the handbook uses one as a hypothesis.
- *Withdrawn* means that the authors took the claim back.
- *Refuted* means that the literature disproves the statement. Spec 06.2.1 keeps such a node in the class claimed until the counterexample is refereed.

Each open problem has five parts. The Statement part gives the statement, its number S1 to S60 in spec 06.2, and its Lean name. The Proof in words part says what is known, dimension by dimension, and gives the idea of the known proofs and reductions. The Examples part gives the first test case of the problem, as spec 11.2.1 asks. The Lean part says what the library mmp-lean states on the date. The Notes and history part gives the claims of 2025 and 2026 with the status of their records, the routes of the project that attack the problem, the references, and some questions for a reader. The sections have the kind survey, so they have no exercises.

The chapters follow the topics of spec 11.2.1.

1. Termination of flips, by dimension, with the claims of 2026 and the cases in dimension 5 and higher.
2. Minimal models, non-vanishing and abundance, with generalized abundance and the Iitaka conjecture.
3. The ACC and the lower semicontinuity of minimal log discrepancies, the mld bound and Target T.
4. Boundedness after BAB, effective results, K-stability and Cremona groups.
5. Positive and mixed characteristic.
6. Foliations and the Kähler program.
7. How the project tracks these problems: the statements, the claim records, the audit, the routes and the gaps of Lean.

The spine of the volume is Birkar's ICM 2018 survey and Xu-Zhuang 2026, as spec 11.2 gives it. The records of the project do not give the title or the arXiv number of Xu-Zhuang 2026, so this report does not cite it for any statement. The volume needs all of Volumes 0 to 16. A reader who has read only Volume 0 can still use the statements and the status tables, and can follow the references to the volume that treats each topic.
