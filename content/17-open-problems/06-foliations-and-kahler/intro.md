# Foliations and the Kähler program

Two settings extend the program beyond projective varieties with a pair. A foliation replaces $K_X$ by the canonical class $K_{\mathcal{F}}$ of a foliation. A compact Kähler manifold replaces the projective variety, and currents replace curves.

Section 6.1 treats foliations: the program for algebraically integrable foliations, the general foliations of dimension at least 4, lc foliated flips and the questions of the survey of Cascini and Spicer. Section 6.2 treats the Kähler program: the complete case of 3-folds, the claims of 2026 in all dimensions, and Kähler non-vanishing.

Route R8 (weight 0.05) attacks foliations, and route R9 (weight 0.05) attacks the Kähler program. Neither route has a Lean statement on 2026-11-26. The project lacks the vocabulary of foliations, and Mathlib has no Kähler manifolds.
