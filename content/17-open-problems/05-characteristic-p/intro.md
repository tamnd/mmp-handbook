# Positive and mixed characteristic

Spec 01.4 names four tools of characteristic 0 that fail or weaken in characteristic $p$: resolution of singularities (open in dimension at least 4), Kawamata-Viehweg vanishing (false in general), the existence of flips by the method of characteristic 0, and generic smoothness. So the program in characteristic $p$ is a separate subject, with its own proofs.

Section 5.1 treats 3-folds: the program for $p \ge 5$, the small primes 2 and 3, and abundance, with the claims of 2026. Section 5.2 treats 4-folds, which need log resolutions, and the program in mixed characteristic.

Route R7 of the project (weight 0.05) attacks this cluster. Spec 04.8 says that the 3-fold program for $p \ge 5$ is complete and that abundance is close, and that the open parts are small primes, 4-folds and moduli. Lab experiment E7 gives the data in characteristic $p$.
