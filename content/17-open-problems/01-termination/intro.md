# Termination of flips

Termination is the conjecture that every sequence of flips is finite. With the existence of flips, it makes the minimal model program run to its end. Flips exist for klt pairs in all dimensions (BCHM) and for lc pairs (Birkar; Hacon-Xu), so on 2026-11-26 termination is the main open step of the program in characteristic 0.

Section 1.1 gives the conjecture and its state in each dimension, as refereed work has it. Section 1.2 gives the claims of 2025 and 2026: the Lean proof for lc 4-folds with rational boundary, the claims for a big boundary, and the claim on lc generalized flips. Section 1.3 treats dimension 5 and higher, where no claim covers the general case.

The spec of the project (03.11) names three entrances to termination. The first is the ACC and the lower semicontinuity of minimal log discrepancies (route R4, Chapter 3). The second is bigness (Hashizume, route R1). The third is the 4-fold proof in Lean (route R2, Section 7.3). This chapter cites the three and gives the details of the second and the third.
