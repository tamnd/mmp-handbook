# Generalized pairs

A generalized pair is a pair $(X, B)$ with one more summand $M$: the push-forward of a nef divisor that lives on a higher birational model. Birkar and Zhang introduced them in 2014 (BZ16) to prove effective Iitaka fibrations. They are now the standard language for boundedness, for the canonical bundle formula and for induction on dimension, because the base of an lc-trivial fibration carries a generalized pair and not a pair.

Section 16.1.1 gives the definitions, proves that a generalized lc pair with $K_X + B$ $\mathbb{R}$-Cartier has $(X, B)$ lc, and works out the first example: the generalized pair on the base of an elliptic fibration, given by Kodaira's canonical bundle formula. Section 16.1.2 reports the generalized program: the refereed existence of klt NQC generalized flips, and the claims of 2026 on lc generalized flips, on finite generation and on b-semiampleness.
