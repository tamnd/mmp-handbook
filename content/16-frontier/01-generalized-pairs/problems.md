## Problems

The problems of this chapter are of grade C. Some ask for a computation with the definitions of Section 16.1.1; the others are questions that the claims of Section 16.1.2 raise, and their answers are not known to the project on 2026-11-28.

1. (C) Let $f \colon X \to C$ be a relatively minimal elliptic surface with no multiple fibre. Prove the identity $12 b_P + \operatorname{ord}_P(\text{pole of } j) = e(F_P)$ for each Kodaira fibre type from a resolution of the fibre and its log canonical threshold, and deduce $\deg(K_C + B_C + \mathbf{M}_C) = 2g - 2 + \chi(\mathcal{O}_X)$. Where does the identity fail for a multiple fibre $m I_n$, and how does the formula of Section 16.1.1 correct it?
2. (C) Find a relatively minimal elliptic surface over $\mathbb{P}^1$ with $\chi(\mathcal{O}_X) = 1$ for which the generalized pair $(\mathbb{P}^1, B_C + \mathbf{M})$ has both a nonzero boundary part and a nef part of positive degree. List all the splittings $\deg B_C + \deg \mathbf{M}_C = 1$ that occur for rational elliptic surfaces with a section, using the classification of their singular fibres.
3. (C) Let $g \colon Y \to X$ be a log resolution and $\mathbf{M}$ a b-divisor that descends to $Y$ with $\mathbf{M}_Y$ nef. Show that the set of $t \ge 0$ for which $(X, B + t\mathbf{M})$ is glc is a closed interval, and compute it for the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at a point with $\mathbf{M}_Y = H - E$. Compare with the example after the lemma of Section 16.1.1.
4. (C) Give an example of a glc generalized pair $(X, B + \mathbf{M})$ with $X$ not $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial and $K_X + B$ not $\mathbb{R}$-Cartier, so that the lemma of Section 16.1.1 does not apply. Can $(X, B)$ fail to be lc in a sense that makes sense without $K_X + B$ being $\mathbb{R}$-Cartier, for instance through a small $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial modification?
5. (C) Run the generalized program for $(\mathbb{F}_n, 0 + \mathbf{M})$ with $\mathbf{M}_{\mathbb{F}_n} = a\sigma_\infty + bF$ nef, where $\sigma_\infty$ is the positive section and $F$ a fibre. For which $(a, b)$ does it contract the negative section, for which does it end with $\mathbb{F}_n \to \mathbb{P}^1$, and for which is $K + \mathbf{M}$ already nef? Draw the regions in the $(a, b)$-plane.
6. (C) The claim C17 (Liu and Wang, arXiv:2608.03258) gives a klt generalized pair with a generalized lc ring that is not finitely generated. Read the example and decide whether its nef part is NQC, whether $K_X + B + \mathbf{M}_X$ is big, and which step of the proof of finite generation for usual pairs fails for it. This is target O-R1-7 of route R1.
