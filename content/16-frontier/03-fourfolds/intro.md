# Termination in dimension 4

Termination of flips is the conjecture that each sequence of flips is finite. In dimension 3 it is a theorem. In dimension 4 it is proved for several classes of pairs, and on 2026-11-28 the general lc case with rational boundary is claimed by an AI-written manuscript and its Lean formalization, which the project audits (Chapter 16.4).

Section 16.3.1 gives what is proved and what is claimed, with the two reductions that the project states in Lean. Section 16.3.2 is a worked example: a toric 4-fold with two curves of quotient singularities, a sequence of two flips, and a hand check that the sequence terminates, by a count of toric valuations of small log discrepancy.
