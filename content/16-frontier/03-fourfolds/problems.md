## Problems

The problems of this chapter are of grade C. The first four use the toric example of Section 16.3.2; the others ask for checks of claims of Section 16.3.1 that the project has not done on 2026-11-28.

1. (C) For the fan of $X_1$ in Section 16.3.2, list the nine maximal cones, compute their indices, and show that the curve $V(\operatorname{Cone}(v_3, v_5, v_6))$ is the only singular locus. Show that $X_1$ is not Fano by finding the curve $\ell$ with $K_{X_1} \cdot \ell > 0$.
2. (C) Replace $v_5 = (0, -2, -3, -1)$ by $v_5' = (0, -a, -b, -1)$ with $0 < a < b$ integers, keeping the other five vectors. Compute the Gale dual, the chambers and the class of $-K$. For which $(a, b)$ is the variety of the chamber containing $-K$ terminal, and how many flips does its $K$-program have?
3. (C) Compute $(-K_{X_0})^4$ for the Fano 4-fold $X_0$ of Section 16.3.2, as $4!$ times the volume of the polytope $\{m \in M_{\mathbb{R}} : \langle m, v_i \rangle \ge -1\}$, and check the answer against the intersection numbers of the divisors $D_\rho$ computed from the fan.
4. (C) Give a sequence of toric 4-fold flips along which the count $d$ of Section 16.3.2 stays the same at one flip, and explain how Theorem 3.6.3 still proves that the sequence is finite.
5. (C) State the reduction of Han and Liu (statement S29) in full, and check against HL25 which hypotheses the statement in Lean leaves out. List the steps that would turn it, together with the ACC of minimal log discrepancies for 4-folds with a given set of coefficients, into termination of flips for 4-folds.
6. (C) Read Section 16.3.1 together with the claim records of C1 and C2. For each of the statements S22, S24 and S25, say which of the others it implies, and which of the implications are proved and which rest on a claim.
