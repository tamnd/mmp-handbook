# Kähler 3-folds

A compact Kähler manifold need not be projective, and then it may have few curves or none. The minimal model program still makes sense for it, with positive closed currents in place of curves. For 3-folds the program is complete: the existence of minimal models and Mori fibre spaces, abundance, log abundance and slc abundance are all refereed or published results on 2026-11-28, as spec 01.5 records. In dimension 4 and higher the program is open, and two preprints of 2026 claim large parts of it.

Section 16.2.1 gives the setting, the theorem for 3-folds and three examples in which the varieties are not projective. Section 16.2.2 gives the claims of 2026 in higher dimension, with their records, and the targets of route R9.
