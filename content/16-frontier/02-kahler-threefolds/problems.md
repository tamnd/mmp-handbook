## Problems

The problems of this chapter are of grade C. They ask for the Kähler forms of projective arguments, or for checks of the claims of Section 16.2.2 that the project has not done on 2026-11-28.

1. (C) Let $T = E \times A$ with $E$ an elliptic curve and $A$ a general complex torus of dimension 2, which has no curves. Show that the curves of $T$ are the translates of $E \times \{a\}$. Let $X$ be the blow-up of $T$ along $E \times \{0\}$. Compute $K_X$, the exceptional divisor and $\overline{NE}(X)$, and run the Kähler program on $X$. Which cone, $\overline{NE}(X)$ or $\overline{NA}(X)$, is needed to see that $K_T$ is nef?
2. (C) Let $S$ be a K3 surface with Picard number 0 and $X = \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{O}_S \oplus L)$ for a topologically trivial line bundle $L$ on $S$. Compute $\overline{NE}(X)$ and decide whether $X$ has any $K_X$-negative ray other than the fibres of $X \to S$.
3. (C) Prove the identity $K_X \cdot \pi^* \omega^2 = 0$ of the first example of Section 16.2.1 and show that the class $\pi^*[\omega]^2 + \epsilon [l]$ lies in $\overline{NA}(X)$ for small $\epsilon > 0$. Find the face of $\overline{NA}(X)$ on which $K_X$ vanishes.
4. (C) Carry out target O-R1-11 for one statement: take Corollary 1.2 of Hacon-Wang, arXiv:2611.29683, list each result of an AI manuscript that its proof uses, and classify each as a theorem, a claim with a record, or a claim with no record. Spec 01.3 says that Corollary 1.2 rests on C3; check this.
5. (C) State Tosatti's transcendental basepoint-free conjecture in the form claimed in C11, and show that for a projective manifold and a rational class it is the basepoint free theorem of Kawamata and Shokurov.
