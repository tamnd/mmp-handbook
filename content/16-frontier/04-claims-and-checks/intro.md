# The claims of 2026 and the project's checks

In 2026 strong results in the minimal model program appeared faster than referees could read them, and several came from AI systems, with a human author or with none. Spec 01.3 of the project lists seventeen of them as claims C1 to C17. This chapter reports them as the claim records of tamnd/mmp give them on 2026-11-28, and reports the two routes of the project that check them.

Section 16.4.1 lists the seventeen claims with their sources and their states. Each is claimed, and one, C16, is withdrawn. Section 16.4.2 reports route R2, the audit of the Lean formalization AxiomMath/MMP, which bears on claim C1. Section 16.4.3 reports route R1, the formal checks of parts of the other claims. On 2026-11-28 neither route has finished a check, and the chapter says what each has and has not done.
