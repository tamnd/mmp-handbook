## Problems

Each problem computes a cone of curves with its extremal rays, or tests a step of the proof of Theorem 6.2.9 on an example.

1. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$. Show that $\overline{NE}(X)$ is spanned by the classes of the three rulings, that each has length $2$, and that $X$ is Fano.
2. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{O} \oplus \mathcal{O}(a))$ over $\mathbb{P}^1$ be the Hirzebruch surface $\mathbb{F}_a$, $a \ge 0$. Use Proposition P7.4.19 to find the extremal rays, decide which are $K_X$-negative, and check Theorem 6.2.9 for each $a$.
3. (A) Let $S$ be a smooth del Pezzo surface of degree $8$ other than $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$. Identify it with a variety of Section 3 and list the extremal rays with their lengths.
4. (B) Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety and $\pi \colon Y \to X$ the blow-up of a point, $n \ge 2$, with exceptional divisor $E$ and a line $e \subseteq E$. Show that $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} [e]$ is a $K_Y$-negative extremal ray of length $n - 1$, using the nef divisor $\pi^* A$ for $A$ ample on $X$.
5. (B) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^n$ along a linear subspace $\Lambda \cong \mathbb{P}^{k}$, $0 \le k \le n - 2$, with exceptional divisor $E$. Show that $\rho(X) = 2$, find the two extremal rays and their contractions, and show that the ray contracted by $X \to \mathbb{P}^n$ has length $n - k - 1$.
6. (B) In Step 1 of the proof of Theorem 6.2.9, show that the curve $C$ cannot be chosen rational in general, and explain why the argument does not need it to be.
7. (B) Let $X$ be smooth projective and $H$ ample. Show that for $\varepsilon \ge n + 1$, every rational curve $\Gamma \in \mathcal{R}$ has $(K_X + \varepsilon H) \cdot \Gamma \ge 0$ when $H$ is integral, and deduce that $K_X + (n + 1) H$ is nef. (This is the nefness that goes with Fujita's conjecture, which asks for $K_X + (n + 1) H$ to be base point free.)
8. (B) Let $X = \operatorname{Bl}_{p_1, p_2} \mathbb{P}^3$ and $\varphi$ the contraction of $\ell_{12}$ from Proposition 6.2.18. Show that $\varphi(X)$ is singular at $\varphi(\ell_{12})$, and that the singularity is analytically the cone over $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$.
9. (C) Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety in characteristic $p > 0$ with $K_X$ not nef. Trace the proof of Theorem 6.2.9 and identify the single step where the Frobenius morphism enters, through Theorem 6.1.2.
10. (C) Let $X$ be a smooth projective threefold containing a surface $E \cong \mathbb{P}^2$ with $N_{E/X} \cong \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(-a)$, $a \ge 1$, and suppose that the ray $R$ spanned by a line $e \subseteq E$ is extremal with a contraction $\varphi \colon X \to Y$ that maps $E$ to a point and is an isomorphism elsewhere. Show that $E \cdot e = -a$ and $-K_X \cdot e = 3 - a$, so $R$ is $K_X$-negative only for $a \in \{1, 2\}$. If $K_Y$ is $\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier, show that $K_X = \varphi^* K_Y + \frac{3 - a}{a} E$, so the discrepancy of $E$ is $2$ for $a = 1$ and $\frac12$ for $a = 2$.
