## Problems

Each problem computes a family of rational curves on an explicit variety and compares it with the dimension estimate, bend-and-break or the length bound of the chapter.

1. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^n$. Show that a rational curve of degree $d \ge 2$ has $-K_X \cdot \Gamma \ge n + 2$, and use Corollary 6.1.4 to write $[\Gamma] = d [L]$ as a sum of classes of lines obtained by repeated breaking.
2. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ and $C$ a smooth curve of bidegree $(1, 2)$. Compute $f^* T_X$ for an isomorphism $f \colon \mathbb{P}^1 \to C$, check that $C$ is very free, and compute the dimension of the family of such curves through two fixed general points.
3. (A) Show that the lines on a smooth quadric surface $Q \subseteq \mathbb{P}^3$ are free, not very free, and unsplit, and that they form two minimal rational components with a single point as VMRT.
4. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^a \times \mathbb{P}^b$. Find all unsplit classes of rational curves, and show that a rational curve of class $[\ell_1] + [\ell_2]$ through two general points exists and is very free.
5. (B) Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^4$ be a smooth cubic threefold. Show that $-K_X = 2H$, that lines have $-K_X \cdot \ell = 2$, and use Lemma 6.1.3 to show that every component of the family of lines has dimension at least $2 + 3 = 5$ in $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{P}^1, X)$, so at least $2$ after dividing by $\operatorname{PGL}_2$. (The Fano surface of lines has dimension exactly $2$.)
6. (B) Let $S$ be a smooth cubic surface and $L$ a line. Show that $L$ is the only integral curve whose class lies on the ray $R_L$, and that every effective $1$-cycle with class in $R_L$ is a multiple of $L$.
7. (B) Let $X = \operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2$ with exceptional curve $e$ and $\tilde\ell$ the strict transform of a line through $p$. Compute $\ell(R)$ for the two extremal rays, decide which classes are unsplit, and compute the minimal rational component and its VMRT at a general point.
8. (B) Let $\Lambda \cong \mathbb{P}^n$ be a linear subspace of $\mathbb{P}^m$ and $f \colon \mathbb{P}^1 \to \Lambda$ the rational normal curve of degree $n$. Show that $T_{\mathbb{P}^m}|_\Lambda \cong T_\Lambda \oplus \mathcal{O}(1)^{\oplus(m - n)}$ and that $f^* T_{\mathbb{P}^m} \cong \mathcal{O}(n + 1)^{\oplus n} \oplus \mathcal{O}(n)^{\oplus(m - n)}$, so that the bound of Proposition 6.1.10 is attained by some summands of a curve that is not a multiple cover.
9. (C) Let $k = \mathbb{C}$ and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^{n+1}$ a general hypersurface of degree $e$ with $2 \le e \le n$. Assume that the lines through a general point form a family of the expected dimension $n - e$. Show that $X$ is covered by lines, and that a general line of $X$ is free with $f^* T_X \cong \mathcal{O}(2) \oplus \mathcal{O}(1)^{\oplus(n - e)} \oplus \mathcal{O}^{\oplus(e - 1)}$.
10. (C) Let $S$ be a smooth cubic surface over $\mathbb{C}$, $L$ and $L'$ two lines that meet, and $\varphi = (\pi_L, \pi_{L'}) \colon S \to \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$. Show that $\varphi$ is generically finite of degree $2$, that it contracts exactly one curve, the third line of the plane spanned by $L$ and $L'$, and that the points $x$ at which the conics of $|H - L|$ and $|H - L'|$ through $x$ are tangent lie on the ramification curve of $\varphi$.
