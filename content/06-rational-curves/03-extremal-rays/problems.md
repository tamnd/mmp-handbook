## Problems

Each problem computes the extremal rays of an explicit variety with their lengths, contractions and minimal curves, or tests the inequalities of the chapter.

1. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$. Find the three extremal rays, their lengths and contractions, and check Corollary 6.3.3 for each.
2. (A) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^3$ at two points. Using Proposition 6.2.18, show that the two rays of $e_1$ and $e_2$ are of type E2 and that the ray of $\ell_{12}$ is not $K_X$-negative, so it is not covered by Theorem 6.3.9.
3. (A) Let $Q \subseteq \mathbb{P}^4$ be a smooth quadric threefold. Show that $\rho(Q) = 1$, that the extremal ray has length $3$, and that the lines through a general point sweep out a surface, in accordance with Example 6.3.6.
4. (B) Let $X = \operatorname{Bl}_C \mathbb{P}^3$ for a smooth conic $C$. Show that $X$ has a ray of type E1 and a second ray whose contraction is the morphism given by the quadrics through $C$, and identify its type.
5. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{O} \oplus \mathcal{O}(1))$ over $\mathbb{P}^2$. Identify $X$ with a variety of Section 3, and find the types of its two rays.
6. (B) Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^2$ be a smooth divisor of bidegree $(1, 2)$. Show that $X$ is a smooth projective surface, that the projection to $\mathbb{P}^2$ is birational, and find the extremal rays of $X$ with their lengths.
7. (B) Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety of dimension $n$ and $V$ an unsplit dominating family with $-K_X \cdot V = n$. Show that $\dim \operatorname{Locus}(V_x) \ge n - 1$ for $x \in \operatorname{ev}_0(V)$, and verify the bound on a smooth quadric of dimension $n$.
8. (B) Let $\varphi \colon X \to Y$ be the contraction of a $K_X$-negative extremal ray of a smooth projective fourfold with $\ell(R) = 3$. Show that $\varphi$ is either of fibre type with fibres of dimension at least $2$, or divisorial with fibres of dimension at least $3$, so that the exceptional divisor maps to a point.
9. (C) Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety of dimension $n$ with a $K_X$-negative extremal ray $R$ of length $\ell(R) = n + 1$ that has a contraction. Show, using Corollary 6.3.3, that the contraction maps $X$ to a point, so $\rho(X) = 1$ and $X$ is Fano.
10. (C) Let $X$ be the Fano threefold $\operatorname{Bl}_C \mathbb{P}^3$ of Example 6.3.20. Show that the minimal curves of the D3 ray, the lines in the planes through $C$, form a dominating family $V$ with $\dim V = 6$ and $\dim V_x = 1 + 2 = 3$ for general $x$, counting the reparametrizations that fix $0$, and check Corollary 6.3.2 for $V$: $\operatorname{Locus}(V_x)$ is the plane through $C$ and $x$.
