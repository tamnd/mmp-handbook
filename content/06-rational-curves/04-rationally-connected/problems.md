## Problems

Each problem decides the rational connectedness of an explicit variety, or computes a quotient by chains or an MRC fibration, using the results of the chapter.

1. (A) Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^{n+1}$ be a smooth cubic hypersurface, $n \ge 2$. Show that $X$ is Fano and that the lines on $X$ form a dominating family for $n \ge 3$ but not for $n = 2$, and deduce from Corollary 6.4.4 that $X$ is rationally connected for $n \ge 3$.
2. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$. Show that any two points are joined by a chain of at most three curves of the three rulings, and that two general points are not joined by fewer.
3. (A) Let $S$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at $r \le 8$ points in general position. Show that $S$ is rationally connected in two ways: by Theorem 6.4.8, and by its rationality.
4. (B) Let $C$ be a smooth projective curve of genus $g \ge 1$ and $X = \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E})$ for a vector bundle $\mathcal{E}$ of rank $r \ge 2$ on $C$. Show that the projection to $C$ is a Mori fibre space and the MRC fibration, and that $X$ is uniruled but not rationally connected.
5. (B) Let $X = S \times \mathbb{P}^1$ for a smooth projective surface $S$ with $K_S$ nef. Show that the projection to $S$ is a Mori fibre space, and use Theorem 6.4.15 and Theorem P8.3.10 to identify the MRC fibration.
6. (B) Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety of dimension $n$ in characteristic $0$ with $\rho(X) = 2$ and two $K_X$-negative extremal rays, one of fibre type and one birational, each with a contraction. Show that $X$ is uniruled, and give an example, among the varieties of Chapter 3, which is rationally connected. Does Proposition 6.4.3 apply directly?
7. (B) Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^3$ be a general divisor of bidegree $(1, 3)$, as in Example 6.3.14. Show that $X$ is rationally connected, using either of its two rays, and identify which results of the chapter each route uses.
8. (B) Let $\varphi \colon X \to Y$ be a Mori fibre space of relative dimension $1$, with $X$ a smooth threefold. Show that every fibre of $\varphi$ is a conic, possibly singular, and check that it is rationally chain connected, as Theorem 6.4.19 predicts.
9. (C) Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety in characteristic $0$ with a dominant rational map $X \dashrightarrow Z$ to a variety $Z$ of general type with $\dim Z > 0$. Show that $X$ is not rationally connected, using Proposition P8.3.14 or Corollary 6.4.16, and show that the MRC fibration of $X$ factors, birationally, through the map to $Z$.
10. (C) Let $X$ be a Fano manifold with $\rho(X) = 1$ of dimension $n$ in characteristic $0$, and $V$ a dominating family of rational curves of minimal anticanonical degree through a general point. Show that two general points are joined by a chain of curves of $V$ of length at most $n$, by showing that the loci of chains of length $j$ through a general point form a strictly increasing sequence of subvarieties until they fill $X$.
