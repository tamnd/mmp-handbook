# Shokurov's 3-fold log flips

This chapter gives Shokurov's proof of the existence of flips of 3-fold klt pairs (Sho92), in the form of Corti's account (Cor07, Chapter 2) and Fujino's account of the reduction (Cor07). It is a second proof of Mori's theorem of Chapter 2, valid for all $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial klt 3-fold pairs, and it does not use the classification of extremal neighbourhoods. Its structure is the one later used by Hacon and McKernan, and by Birkar, Cascini, Hacon and McKernan, in every dimension.

The proof has two halves. Section 1 treats the reduction. A *pl flipping contraction* is a flipping contraction of a plt pair $(X, S + B)$ on which the boundary component $S$ is negative. By Theorem 8.1.6 its flip is the relative Proj of $R(X/Z, S)$, and an elementary argument shows that this algebra is finitely generated if and only if its *restricted algebra*, the image of restriction to $S$, is. That is a question on the surface $S$. Shokurov's reduction theorem says that pl flips and special termination together give all klt flips, and special termination for 3-folds comes from the fact that surfaces have no flips. The Francia flip is a pl flip, and on its plt centre it restricts to a weighted blow-up of a smooth surface point.

Section 2 states Shokurov's theorem on 3-fold pl flips and outlines its proof: the restricted algebra is identified with an algebra of mobile parts of divisors on the surface $S$ by Kawamata-Viehweg vanishing, and these mobile parts stabilise, by the theory of complements on surfaces. The existence of 3-fold klt flips follows. The section ends with the definition of complements and the simplest examples.
