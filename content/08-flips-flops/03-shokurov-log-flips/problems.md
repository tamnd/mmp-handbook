## Problems

Each problem computes a restricted algebra, a pl flipping contraction on a toric model, or a complement on a curve or a surface germ.

1. (A) Check that the family of Example 8.1.14 consists of pl flipping contractions for $S = D_1$: compute $D_1 \cdot C^-$ and $(K_{X^-} + D_1) \cdot C^-$ for every $n \ge 2$.
2. (A) For the family of Problem 1, identify the surface $S = D_1$, its singular point and the morphism $S \to T$ as a weighted blow-up of $\mathbb{A}^2$, and compute $(C^-)^2_S$ and $K_S \cdot C^-$.
3. (A) Let $T = \mathbb{P}^1$ and $B = \frac12 p_1 + \frac34 p_2 + \frac34 p_3$. Find the least $n$ for which $K_T + B$ has an $n$-complement.
4. (A) Show that the pair $(\mathbb{A}^2, \{y^2 = x^3\})$ is not lc, and that $(\mathbb{A}^2, \frac56 \{y^2 = x^3\})$ is lc, using the minimal log resolution of the cusp.
5. (B) Find a $1$-complement or a $2$-complement of the germ $(\mathbb{A}^2, \frac12 C)$ with $C$ the cuspidal cubic $\{y^2 = x^3\}$, or show that none exists for $n \le 2$.
6. (B) Let $R = A[\sigma, x, y]/(xy - a\sigma^3)$ with $a \in A$, graded by $\deg \sigma = 1$, $\deg x = 1$ and $\deg y = 2$. Compute $R / \sigma R$, check Theorem 8.3.4, and describe $\operatorname{Proj} R$ over $\operatorname{Spec} A$ when $A = k[a]$.
7. (B) In the Francia flip, compute the restricted algebra $R_S$ for $S = D_1$ in degrees $m \le 4$ as submodules of $k(S)$, using the toric description of $\mathcal{O}_{X^-}(mD_1)$ by lattice points.
8. (B) For the dlt pair $(X^-, D_1 + D_2)$ on the Francia 3-fold, compute the lc centres, and decide whether the flip of $C^-$ contains any of them.
9. (C) Let $S \to T = \mathbb{A}^2$ be the weighted blow-up with weights $(1, n)$ on the coordinates $(x, y)$, with exceptional curve $E$. Show that $H^0(S, \mathcal{O}_S(\lfloor -mE \rfloor))$ is the ideal $I_m$ spanned by the monomials $x^i y^j$ with $i + nj \ge m$, and that $\bigoplus_m I_m$ is generated in degrees at most $n$ and no fewer.
