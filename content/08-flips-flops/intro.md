# Flips, flops and the 3-fold MMP

This volume constructs the minimal model program in dimension 3. Chapters 1 to 3 define flips and flops and prove their existence for terminal 3-folds and for 3-fold log pairs. Chapter 4 proves that sequences of flips terminate, Chapter 5 assembles the program and proves that flops connect minimal models, and Chapter 6 outlines the semistable minimal model program.
