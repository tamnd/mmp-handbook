# The semistable MMP

This chapter runs the 3-fold program of Chapter 5 for a family of surfaces over a curve, and ends the volume with a summary and pointers to Volumes 9 and 10. The ground field is algebraically closed of characteristic $0$.

Section 1 defines semistable families and recalls the semistable reduction theorem of Kempf, Knudsen, Mumford and Saint-Donat. For a semistable family $X \to T$ of surfaces the $K_X$-MMP over $T$ is a case of Theorem 8.5.3; it keeps the fibres reduced and changes only finitely many of them when the general fibre has $K$ nef. The relative Zariski lemma, proved in full, shows that a nef divisor supported on a fibre is a multiple of the fibre, and it gives the main application: if the canonical class of the general fibre is trivial, the output of the program has $K \sim_{\mathbb{Q}} 0$ over the base. The construction of the flips that occur by the semistable structure alone, due to Kawamata and Kollár and Mori, is outlined and marked as an outline, and the theorem of Kulikov and of Persson and Pinkham on degenerations of K3 surfaces is cited. The examples are the degenerations of a quartic surface to a tetrahedron and to two quadrics, and the blow-up of a point of a fibre of a family of curves.

Section 2 summarizes the volume and points ahead: to Volume 9 for the existence of flips in all dimensions (BCHM10), pl flips and the MMP with scaling, and to Volume 10 for abundance for 3-folds, termination in dimension 4 and the Sarkisov program.

The sources are KM98, Chapter 7, Kawamata (Kaw88), KKMS73, Kulikov (Kul77) and Persson and Pinkham (PP81).
