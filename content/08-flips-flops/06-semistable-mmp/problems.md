## Problems

Each problem runs the semistable program on an explicit degeneration, applies the relative Zariski lemma, or revisits the volume as a whole.

1. (A) Let $X = \{x_0 x_1 x_2 + t F = 0\} \subset \mathbb{P}^2 \times \Delta$ with $F$ a general cubic form. Show that the total space is smooth, so that $X \to \Delta$ is already a semistable family of elliptic curves, with central fibre a triangle of lines. Explain why the analogous family of Example 8.6.8 has a singular total space.
2. (A) Let $S \to T$ be a semistable family of curves and $C$ a component of $S_0$ with $C \cong \mathbb{P}^1$ meeting the rest of $S_0$ in one point. Show that $C \cdot C = -1$ and that the $K_S$-MMP over $T$ contracts $C$.
3. (A) Check the triple point formula $(D_{ij}|_{V_i})^2 + (D_{ij}|_{V_j})^2 = -T_{ij}$ in Example 8.6.8, where $T_{ij}$ is the number of triple points on the double curve $D_{ij}$.
4. (B) Let $f \colon X \to T$ be semistable with $\dim X = 3$, and suppose that $K_{X_t}$ is nef for every smooth fibre $X_t$. Show that the exceptional locus of every step of the program of Theorem 8.6.4 lies over the points $t$ with $X_t$ singular.
5. (B) In Example 8.6.9, choose a small resolution and identify the two components of the central fibre as quadrics, one of them blown up at some of the $16$ points. Show that the two possible choices at a point differ by an Atiyah flop.
6. (B) Let $X \to T$ be a semistable family of K3 surfaces over a germ with $K_X \sim \sum a_i F_i$, $X_0 = \sum F_i$. Show directly that if some $a_i$ differs from the others, $K_X$ is not nef over $T$, and find a $K$-negative curve.
7. (B) Show that the conclusion of Corollary 8.6.5 holds for any family with $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial terminal total space and reduced fibres, not only for semistable ones.
8. (C) Let $X \to \Delta$ be the family of Example 8.6.8 after a base change $s^2 = t$. Show that the new total space has singular points along the double curves of the central fibre, and describe a semistable model after resolving them.
9. (C) Let $X \to \Delta$ be a Type II Kulikov model. Show that the components $V_1, \dots, V_{k-1}$ are elliptic ruled surfaces whose two double curves are disjoint sections, and that $K_{V_i} = -(D_{i-1,i} + D_{i,i+1})$.
10. (C) Write a one-page account of the 3-fold program for a smooth projective 3-fold, citing for each step the theorem of this volume or of Volumes 3, 4 and 7 that it uses, and mark which of them are proved in full here.
