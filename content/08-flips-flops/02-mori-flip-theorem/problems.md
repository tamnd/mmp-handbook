## Problems

Each problem computes the index, the intersection numbers or the general elephant along an explicit flipping curve, or checks a step of Section 1 on an example.

1. (A) For the Francia flip, compute $h^0$ and $h^1$ of $\mathcal{O}_{X_n}$ for the thickenings $X_n$ of $C^-$ defined by $\mathfrak{m}_z^n \mathcal{O}_{X^-}$, for $n = 1, 2$, on the toric chart that contains the smooth torus fixed point.
2. (A) Let $X$ be a terminal 3-fold and $C$ an irreducible flipping curve through points $P_1, \dots, P_s$ of indices $m_1, \dots, m_s$. Show that $-K_X \cdot C \in \frac1{\operatorname{lcm}(m_1, \dots, m_s)} \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$.
3. (A) Show that the circuit $u_1 + u_2 - u_3 - u_4 = 0$ gives no flipping contraction, and relate this to Proposition 8.2.3.
4. (A) Compute the general elephant at the terminal point $\frac15(1, 4, 2)$ and identify its Du Val type.
5. (B) Let $X$ be the toric 3-fold of the circuit $u_1 + u_2 - u_3 - 3u_4 = 0$ from Example 8.1.14. Write down $-K_{X^-}$ as a sum of torus invariant divisors, compute $-K_{X^-} \cdot C^-$, and describe a general member of $|-K_{X^-}|$ near the singular point.
6. (B) For the same $X^-$, show that the general elephant does not contain $C^-$, and compute the singularities of its image in $Z$.
7. (B) Let $C \subset X$ be a smooth rational curve on a smooth 3-fold with $N_{C/X} = \mathcal{O}(a) \oplus \mathcal{O}(b)$. Show that $-K_X \cdot C = a + b + 2$, compute $h^0(N_{C/X})$ when $a, b \ge -1$, and deduce that if $-K_X \cdot C \ge 1$ then $C$ moves in a family that covers at least a surface.
8. (B) Use Problem 7 to compute $-K \cdot C^+$ and $h^0(N_{C^+/X^+})$ for the flipped curve of Example 8.1.14, with normal bundle $\mathcal{O}(-1) \oplus \mathcal{O}(-n)$, and show that $C^+$ does not move in $X^+$.
9. (C) Let $N$ have basis $u_2, u_3, u_4$, let $u_1 = -u_2 + 2u_3 + 3u_4$, and let $X$ be the toric 3-fold of the cones $(u_1, u_2, u_3)$ and $(u_1, u_2, u_4)$, with $C = V(\operatorname{Cone}(u_1, u_2))$. Compute the singular points of $X$ and their types, decide whether they are terminal, and compute $K_X \cdot C$.
