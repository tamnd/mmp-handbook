## Problems

Each problem runs a step of the program by hand, computes a terminal model, or works out a flop in coordinates.

1. (A) Let $X = \operatorname{Bl}_{p, q} \mathbb{P}^3$ be the blow-up at two distinct points. Find the $K_X$-negative extremal rays and describe every $K_X$-MMP starting at $X$, with its output.
2. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^2$. Show that $X$ has two extremal rays, both of fibre type, and that the $K_X$-MMP of $X$ stops at once with one of two Mori fibre spaces.
3. (A) Show that the point $\frac12(1,1,1)$ is $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial and that the ordinary double point is not, using Example 8.5.14.
4. (B) Let $X$ be a smooth projective 3-fold and $\pi \colon X \to X'$ the contraction of a smooth rational curve $C$ with $N_{C/X} \cong \mathcal{O}(-1)^{\oplus 2}$. Show that $\pi$ is not a step of any $K_X$-MMP, and that $X'$ is not $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial.
5. (B) Let $X = \{xy = z^2 - w^{2k}\} \subset \mathbb{A}^4$ with $k \ge 1$. Show that $X$ is terminal, and construct a $\mathbb{Q}$-factorialization of $X$ by the method of Proposition 8.5.24.
6. (B) Let $X = \{xy = z^3 - w^3\}$, a $cA_2$ point. Show that $X$ is terminal, find three Weil divisors through the origin that are not $\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier, and construct a small resolution with two exceptional curves.
7. (B) Let $X$ be a canonical 3-fold with one singular point, of type $\frac13(1,1,1)$. Describe the crepant terminal model of $X$ and show, with Proposition 8.5.12, that it is unique up to isomorphism over $X$.
8. (C) Let $X$ be a smooth projective 3-fold, $C \subset X$ a curve with $C \cong \mathbb{P}^1$ and $N_{C/X} \cong \mathcal{O}(-1)^{\oplus 2}$, and suppose that $C$ is contracted by a morphism $X \to Z$ to a projective variety that is an isomorphism elsewhere. Show that the flop $X_1$ of $C$ is smooth and projective, and that $X$ and $X_1$ have the same Betti numbers.
9. (C) Let $X_1$ and $X_2$ be two minimal models over $S$ that are $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial and terminal. Show that the set of $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial minimal models isomorphic to $X_1$ in codimension one up to isomorphism over $S$ corresponds to certain open chambers in the movable cone of $X_1$ over $S$, and identify the chambers for the Atiyah flop over $Z$.
10. (C) Let $g \colon Y_n \to X_n$ be the pagoda of Proposition 8.5.24. Show that every effective divisor $G$ on $Y_n$ with $G \cdot C < 0$ contains $C$, and that for any two $\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier divisors $D_1$, $D_2$ on $Y_n$ with $D_1 \cdot C < 0$ and $D_2 \cdot C < 0$ the $D_1$-flop and the $D_2$-flop of $g$ are the same, so that $g$ has exactly one flop.
