## Problems

Each problem computes a relative algebra and its Proj, a flip or flop on an explicit model, or the discrepancies on the two sides of a flip.

1. (A) Let $Z = \{xy = zw\} \subset \mathbb{A}^4$ and $D_Z = \{x = w = 0\}$. Compute $\mathcal{O}_Z(mD_Z)$ for $m \ge 0$, show that $R(Z/Z, D_Z)$ is generated in degree $1$ by two elements, and identify its Proj with one of the two small resolutions of Proposition 8.1.11.
2. (A) Let $Z = \frac12(1,1,1)$ and $D_Z$ the image of a plane through the origin. Compute $\operatorname{Proj} \bigoplus_{m \ge 0} \mathcal{O}_Z(mD_Z)$ and the least $d$ for which $\mathcal{O}_Z(dD_Z)$ is invertible.
3. (A) Let $f \colon X \to \mathbb{A}^2$ be the blow-up of the origin, with exceptional curve $E$. Compute $R(X/\mathbb{A}^2, -2E)$ and its Proj, and show that it is isomorphic to $X$ over $\mathbb{A}^2$.
4. (A) On the fan of the Francia flip (Proposition 8.1.13), compute $D_i \cdot C^+$ and $D_i \cdot C^-$ for $i = 1, \dots, 4$, and check that the strict transform of every $D_i$ with $D_i \cdot C^- < 0$ is positive on $C^+$.
5. (A) For the circuit $u_1 + u_2 - u_3 - 3u_4 = 0$ of Example 8.1.14, compute the type of the singular point of $X^-$, check that it is terminal, and compute $K_{X^\pm} \cdot C^\pm$.
6. (B) Show that the circuit $u_1 + u_2 + u_3 - u_4 - u_5 = 0$ in $\mathbb{Z}^4$ gives a smooth 4-fold $X^-$ with exceptional locus $\mathbb{P}^2$ and a smooth $X^+$ with exceptional locus $\mathbb{P}^1$, compute $K \cdot \ell$ for a line $\ell$ in $\mathbb{P}^2$ and $K \cdot C^+$ for the flipped curve, and decide which direction is the flip.
7. (B) For the 4-fold flip of Problem 6, compute the discrepancy over both sides of the divisor obtained by blowing up the exceptional $\mathbb{P}^2$ of $X^-$, and check Lemma 8.1.19.
8. (B) Show that the affine toric variety $Z$ of Problem 6 is the cone over $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^2$ in its Segre embedding, and that $X^-$ and $X^+$ are the total spaces of $\mathcal{O}(-1)^{\oplus 2}$ over $\mathbb{P}^2$ and of $\mathcal{O}(-1)^{\oplus 3}$ over $\mathbb{P}^1$.
9. (B) In the Francia flip, show that $(X^-, \frac12 D_3)$ is klt, that $K_{X^-} + \frac12 D_3$ is still negative on $C^-$, and compute the flipped pair $(X^+, \frac12 D_3^+)$ and the discrepancy of $D_v$ on both sides.
10. (C) Show that the Atiyah flop $X_1 \dashrightarrow X_2$ is the flop of every divisor $D$ on $X_1$ with $D \cdot C_1 < 0$, and compute $R(X_1/Z, D)$ for $D = \Pi_1' - \Pi_2'$, which has $D \cdot C_1 = -2$, as an explicit quotient of a polynomial ring over $k[Z]$.
