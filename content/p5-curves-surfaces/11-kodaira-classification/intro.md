# Kodaira dimension and the Enriques-Kodaira classification

The plurigenera $P_n(S) = h^0(S, nK_S)$ of a smooth projective surface are birational invariants, and their rate of growth, the Kodaira dimension $\kappa(S) \in \{-\infty, 0, 1, 2\}$, is the coarsest invariant of $S$ up to birational equivalence. This chapter defines it, proves its birational invariance from the blow-up formulas of Chapter 6 and the factorization of Chapter 7, and states the classification of surfaces by Kodaira dimension.

Section 11.1 defines the Iitaka dimension of a divisor on a curve or a surface by the growth of $h^0(mD)$, proves that it takes the values $-\infty, 0, 1, 2$ and that $D^2 > 0$ forces the maximal value, and computes the plurigenera of curves, of products of curves, of surfaces in $\mathbb{P}^3$, of abelian and K3 surfaces. Section 11.2 proves that every surface has a minimal model, reached by contracting $(-1)$-curves in at most $\rho(S) - 1$ steps, that when $\kappa \geq 0$ a surface is minimal exactly when $K$ is nef, and that the minimal model is then unique and every birational map to it is a morphism. Section 11.3 proves the numerical part of the classification, $K^2 = 0$ for $\kappa \in \{0, 1\}$ and $K^2 > 0$ for $\kappa = 2$ on a minimal surface, constructs an Enriques surface as a free quotient of a K3 surface, and states the Enriques-Kodaira classification in every characteristic, with Castelnuovo's criterion, the table of invariants, and the quasi-hyperelliptic and quasi-elliptic surfaces of characteristic $2$ and $3$.

In the minimal model program, Section 11.2 is the surface case of the program itself: running it on a surface contracts $(-1)$-curves until $K$ is nef or the surface is a Mori fibre space, $\mathbb{P}^2$ or a ruled surface. The classification of Section 11.3 is abundance for surfaces, the statement that $K$ nef implies $K$ semiample, and the dichotomy between $\kappa = -\infty$ and $\kappa \geq 0$ is the surface case of the conjecture that a variety is uniruled exactly when it has $\kappa = -\infty$. Chapter 12 recovers the first step from the cone theorem.

The chapter follows Hartshorne, Sections V.5 and V.6, Beauville, Chapters II and VI to X, Reid's *Chapters on algebraic surfaces*, and Lazarsfeld, Section 2.1, for the Iitaka dimension.
