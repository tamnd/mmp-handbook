## Problems

Each problem is a computation with the tools of this chapter: intersection numbers on curves over the base, the classes of fibres, and Theorem 1.2.30.

1. (A) Let $X = \operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2 \cong F_1$ (Theorem P3.11.26), with $\pi \colon X \to \mathbb{P}^2$ the blow-up and $q \colon X \to \mathbb{P}^1$ the projection from $p$, whose fibres $F$ are the strict transforms of the lines through $p$. Show that $E \cdot E = -1$, $E \cdot F = 1$ and $F \cdot F = 0$, that $N_1(X/\mathbb{P}^2) = \mathbb{R}[E]$ and $N_1(X/\mathbb{P}^1) = \mathbb{R}[F]$ are two different lines in $N_1(X)$, and that $-E$ is $\pi$-ample and not $q$-ample, while $E$ is $q$-ample and not $\pi$-ample. With $K_X = \pi^* K_{\mathbb{P}^2} + E$ (Theorem P5.6.3), compute $K_X \cdot E$ and $K_X \cdot F$, and show that $-K_X$ is ample over both bases.
2. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^m \times \mathbb{P}^n$ with $f = p_2$, and use $\operatorname{Pic}(X) = \mathbb{Z}\,\mathcal{O}(1, 0) \oplus \mathbb{Z}\,\mathcal{O}(0, 1)$. Show that every curve over $\mathbb{P}^n$ has class $d [\ell]$ in $N_1(X/\mathbb{P}^n)$ for a line $\ell$ in a fibre and an integer $d \ge 1$, that $\rho(X/\mathbb{P}^n) = 1$, and that $\mathcal{O}(a, b)$ is $f$-ample if and only if $a > 0$. Show that $\mathcal{O}(a, b) \equiv_{\mathbb{P}^n} \mathcal{O}(a', b')$ if and only if $\mathcal{O}(a, b) \sim_{\mathbb{P}^n} \mathcal{O}(a', b')$.
3. (B) Let $M \subset \mathbb{P}^3$ be a line and $\pi \colon X \to \mathbb{P}^3$ the blow-up of $M$, with exceptional divisor $E$ and fibres $f_m = \pi^{-1}(m) \cong \mathbb{P}^1$ for $m \in M$. Show that the curves over $\mathbb{P}^3$ are the curves in the fibres $f_m$, that all $f_m$ have the same class $[f]$, and that for a plane $H \supset M$, $\pi^* H = \tilde H + E$ with $\tilde H \cdot f = 1$. Deduce $E \cdot f = -1$, $\rho(X/\mathbb{P}^3) = 1$, and that $-E$ is $\pi$-ample. Using Lemma 4.1.20, $K_X = \pi^* K_{\mathbb{P}^3} + E$; compute $K_X \cdot f$.
4. (B) Let $f \colon X \to Y$ and $g \colon Y \to Z$ be projective morphisms of quasi-projective varieties. Show that every curve over $Y$ is a curve over $Z$ for $g \circ f$, that the induced map $N_1(X/Y) \to N_1(X/Z)$ is injective and maps $\overline{NE}(X/Y)$ into $\overline{NE}(X/Z)$, and that a $(g \circ f)$-ample element of $\operatorname{Pic}(X)_\mathbb{R}$ is $f$-ample. Check this for $\operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2 \to \mathbb{P}^2 \to \operatorname{Spec} k$, and give an $f$-ample line bundle there that is not ample.
5. (B) Let $q \colon F_1 \to \mathbb{P}^1$ be as in Problem 1, $F_0$ the fibre over a point $c_0$, $x \in F_0$ a point not on the curve $C_- = E$, and $\sigma \colon X \to F_1$ the blow-up of $x$ with exceptional curve $G$. Let $h = q \circ \sigma$ and $F'_0$ the strict transform of $F_0$. Show that $h^*[c_0] = F'_0 + G$, that $F'_0 \cdot F'_0 = G \cdot G = -1$ and $F'_0 \cdot G = 1$, and that every fibre $F_c$ with $c \ne c_0$ has $[F_c] = [F'_0] + [G]$ in $N_1(X/\mathbb{P}^1)$. Using the strict transform $\tilde C_-$, with $\tilde C_- \cdot F'_0 = 1$ and $\tilde C_- \cdot G = 0$, show that $\rho(X/\mathbb{P}^1) = 2$ and $\overline{NE}(X/\mathbb{P}^1) = \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}[F'_0] + \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}[G]$. Decide which of $\tilde C_-$, $\tilde C_- + G$ and $-K_X$ are $h$-ample.
6. (A) Let $E$ be an elliptic curve with origin $O$, $P$ a point of order 3 in the group law, $X = E \times E$ with $f = p_2$, and $L = p_1^* \mathcal{O}_E(P - O)$. Show that $L \equiv_E 0$, that $L$ and $L^2$ are not $\sim_E 0$, that $L^3 \cong \mathcal{O}_X$, and so $L \sim_{\mathbb{Q}, E} 0$.
7. (B) In Example 1.2.23, let $D = a\Delta + b f_1 + c F$ with real $a, b, c$, where $F = E \times \{O\}$. Show that $D$ is $p_2$-ample if and only if $a + b > 0$, and that $D \equiv_E 0$ if and only if $a + b = 0$. Using $F = p_2^*[O]$ and the curves $\Delta$ and $f_1$, show that $D \sim_{\mathbb{R}, E} 0$ if and only if $a = b = 0$.
8. (A) Let $g \colon \mathbb{P}^1 \to \mathbb{P}^1$, $t \mapsto t^2$. Show that $\rho(\mathbb{P}^1/\mathbb{P}^1) = 0$ for $g$ and that $\mathcal{O}(-1)$ is $g$-ample, although it is not ample. Check $g$-ampleness of $\mathcal{O}(-1)$ directly on the affine open sets $\{t \ne \infty\}$ and $\{t \ne 0\}$ of the target.
9. (B) Let $S = \{xy = z^2\} \subset \mathbb{A}^3$ and $L = \{x = z = 0\}$ as in Example 1.2.3, and let $\pi \colon X \to S$ be the blow-up of the vertex. In the chart $y = x y'$, $z = x z'$ of the blow-up of $\mathbb{A}^3$, show that $X$ is given by $y' = z'^2$, with coordinates $(x, z')$ and $E = \{x = 0\}$, and in the chart $x = y x''$, $z = y z''$, by $x'' = z''^2$, with coordinates $(y, z'')$ and $E = \{y = 0\}$. Show that $X$ is smooth, $E \cong \mathbb{P}^1$, and $\operatorname{div}(\pi^* x) = E + 2\tilde L$. Compute the degree of the section $x$ of $\mathcal{I} \mathcal{O}_X = \mathcal{O}_X(-E)$ on $E$ to get $E \cdot E = -2$, and deduce $\tilde L \cdot E = 1$, $\rho(X/S) = 1$, and that $-E$ and $\tilde L$ are $\pi$-ample.
10. (C) Let $Z = \{xy = zw\} \subset \mathbb{A}^4$, $\Pi = \{x = z = 0\}$ and $\Pi' = \{x = w = 0\}$, two planes with $\operatorname{div}(x) = \Pi + \Pi'$. Let $\pi \colon X \to Z$ be the blow-up of the ideal $I = (x, z)$. Show that $X$ is covered by the charts $z = xu$, $y = uw$ with coordinates $(x, u, w)$ and $x = zv$, $w = vy$ with coordinates $(z, v, y)$, glued by $uv = 1$, so that $X$ is smooth, and that $\pi$ is an isomorphism over $Z \setminus \{0\}$ with $C = \pi^{-1}(0) \cong \mathbb{P}^1$, the curve $x = w = 0$ in the first chart. Show that $I \mathcal{O}_X = \mathcal{O}_X(-\tilde \Pi)$ for the strict transform $\tilde \Pi$, generated by $x$ and by $z$ in the two charts. Compute the degree of the section $x$ of $I\mathcal{O}_X$ on $C$, and deduce $\tilde \Pi \cdot C = -1$ and $\tilde \Pi' \cdot C = 1$. Conclude that $\rho(X/Z) = 1$, that $\tilde \Pi'$ is $\pi$-ample and $\tilde \Pi$ is not, and that $\Pi$ is not $\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier on $Z$, since $\pi$ has no exceptional divisor and so $\pi^*(m\Pi) = m \tilde \Pi$ for a Cartier $m\Pi$.
