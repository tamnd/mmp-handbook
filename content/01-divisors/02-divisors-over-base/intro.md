# Divisors and curves over a base

The minimal model program runs over a base. A step of the program is a contraction $X \to Z$, and the next step is decided by the curves that this contraction collapses and by the divisors that are positive on them. So the basic objects of the program are relative: divisors on $X$ up to equivalence over $Z$, curves of $X$ that map to points of $Z$, and the cone that these curves span. This chapter sets up these objects and proves the criterion that connects them, Kleiman's criterion over a base.

The chapter works over an algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic 0, and $f \colon X \to Z$ is a projective morphism of quasi-projective varieties. Section 2.1 defines $\mathbb{Q}$- and $\mathbb{R}$-divisors, $\mathbb{Q}$- and $\mathbb{R}$-Cartier divisors, and linear, $\mathbb{Q}$-linear and $\mathbb{R}$-linear equivalence over $Z$, and it proves that the rational and the real notions agree on rational divisors. Section 2.2 defines the intersection number of a line bundle with a curve that $f$ contracts, the spaces $N^1(X/Z)$ and $N_1(X/Z)$ and the relative cone of curves $NE(X/Z)$, and it proves that $N^1(X/Z)$ has finite dimension, by a reduction to the theorem of the base on a resolution of a projective closure. Section 2.3 defines $f$-ample and $f$-nef divisors and proves the relative Kleiman criterion from the absolute one, Theorem 1.1.9: an $\mathbb{R}$-Cartier divisor is $f$-ample if and only if it is positive on $\overline{NE}(X/Z) \setminus \{0\}$.

The worked examples are the blow-up of a point of $\mathbb{P}^n$ over $\mathbb{P}^n$, where $N^1$ has dimension 1 and the cone is spanned by a line in the exceptional divisor; a $\mathbb{P}^1$-bundle over a curve, where numerical and linear equivalence over the base agree; and $E \times E$ over $E$, where a line bundle is numerically trivial over $E$ but not $\mathbb{R}$-linearly trivial over $E$. The problems compute relative cones and relative ampleness for products, Hirzebruch surfaces, blow-ups and the small resolution of the cone $xy = zw$.

The chapter needs Weil and Cartier divisors (P3, Chapters 8 and 9), ample line bundles (P3, Chapter 6), cohomology and base change (P4, Chapter 5) and the intersection theory of curves and surfaces (P5, Chapters 1, 5 and 12). Chapter 3 uses it for the cones of divisors over a base.
