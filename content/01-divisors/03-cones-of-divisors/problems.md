## Problems

Each problem is a computation by hand with the cones and volume functions of this chapter. The surfaces are $\operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2$ with classes $\ell, E$, the plane blown up at two points with classes $\ell, e_1, e_2$, the Hirzebruch surfaces $\mathbb{F}_n$ with classes $C_-, F$, and $E \times E$ with the coordinates $(s, t, c)$ of Section 3.

1. (A) Let $X$ be the plane blown up at two distinct points, with $L = \ell - e_1 - e_2$ the strict transform of the line through them. Using $\overline{NE}(X) = \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} e_1 + \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} e_2 + \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} L$ (Example P5.12.17), show that $\operatorname{Nef}(X)$ is spanned by $\ell$, $\ell - e_1$ and $\ell - e_2$. Decide whether $2\ell - e_1 - e_2$ and $3\ell - 2e_1 - e_2$ are ample, and for the one that is nef but not ample name the curve on which it vanishes.
2. (A) On $\operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2$ let $D = \ell + 2E$ and $A = 2\ell - E$. Find the smallest $m \ge 1$ for which $mD - A$ is linearly equivalent to an effective divisor, and check that it is effective for every larger $m$.
3. (A) On $\operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2$ show that $h^0(-mK) = (2m + 1)^2$ by counting plane curves of degree $3m$ with multiplicity at least $m$ at $p$, assuming that the $m(m + 1)/2$ conditions are independent. Deduce $\operatorname{vol}(-K) = 8 = K^2$.
4. (B) On the plane blown up at two points let $D = 3\ell - 2e_1 - 2e_2$. Show that $D \cdot L = -1$, that the Zariski decomposition is $P = 2\ell - e_1 - e_2$, $N = L$, and that $\operatorname{vol}(D) = 2$ while $D^2 = 1$.
5. (B) On $\mathbb{F}_3$ compute the Zariski decomposition of $-K = 2C_- + 5F$, and check that $\operatorname{vol}(-K) = 25/3$. Show that $-K$ is big and not nef, and that the negative part of $-mK$ is $\frac{m}{3} C_-$, which is integral exactly when $3$ divides $m$. Using $h^0(aC_- + bF) = \sum_{i=0}^{a} \max(0, b - 3i + 1)$ for $a, b \ge 0$, check that $h^0(-3K) = h^0(5C_- + 15F) = 51$.
6. (B) Let $\pi \colon \operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2 \to \mathbb{P}^1$ be the ruling by the lines through $p$, with fibre class $\ell - E$. Show that $N^1(X/\mathbb{P}^1) \cong \mathbb{R}$ by $a\ell - bE \mapsto a - b$, and compute $\operatorname{Nef}$, $\operatorname{Amp}$, $\operatorname{Eff}$ and $\operatorname{Big}$ over $\mathbb{P}^1$. Show that the exceptional curve $E$ is ample over $\mathbb{P}^1$.
7. (B) On $\mathbb{F}_n$ restrict the volume to the line $a = 1$, that is to the classes $C_- + bF$ with $b \ge 0$. Show that $\operatorname{vol}(C_- + bF)$ is $b^2/n$ for $0 \le b \le n$ and $2b - n$ for $b \ge n$, and that this function is continuously differentiable at $b = n$ but not twice differentiable there.
8. (B) On $S = E \times E$ with $E$ without complex multiplication, find all real $\lambda$ for which $f_1 + f_2 + \lambda \Delta$ is ample, nef, and pseudoeffective. Show that the boundary value of $\lambda$ gives a multiple of the class of the antidiagonal.
9. (B) On $S = E \times E$ use the formula $x \cdot x' = st' + s't - 2cc'$ to show that the graphs of $[n]$ and $[m]$ satisfy $\Gamma_n \cdot \Gamma_m = (n - m)^2$. Explain the answer as the number of points $P$ with $nP = mP$.
10. (C) On $\operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2$ show that $\operatorname{vol}^{1/2}$ is concave on $\operatorname{Big}(X)$, using the formula of Example 1.3.19: check that each of the two pieces $\sqrt{a^2 - b^2}$ and $a$ is concave on its region, and that the function is continuously differentiable across the ray $b = 0$.
