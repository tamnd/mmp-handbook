## Problems

Each problem is a computation by hand with the tools of this chapter: charts of blow-ups as in Theorem P3.11.4, the Jacobian form of $K_{X/Y}$ in Theorem 1.5.13, the formula $K = \pi^* K + (c - 1)E$ of Proposition 1.5.14, and the intersection numbers of Example 1.5.15.

1. (A) Let $\pi \colon Y \to \mathbb{P}^3$ be the blow-up of a point, with $H = \pi^* H$ and $E$ as in Example 1.5.15. Expand $(4H - 2E)^3$ term by term and show $(-K_Y)^3 = 56$. Show that $(-K_Y)^2 \cdot E = 4$, and check it by computing $-K_Y|_E \cong \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(2)$ from $K_Y = -4H + 2E$ and $\mathcal{O}_E(E) \cong \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(-1)$. Use adjunction (Theorem P3.12.30) to check that $K_E = (K_Y + E)|_E \cong \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^2}(-3)$.
2. (A) In Example 1.5.15, show that $(H - E) \cdot E^{n-1} = (-1)^n$ and $(H - E)^{n-1} \cdot E = 1$. For $n = 4$, compute $E^4$ and $(-K_Y)^4 = 625 - 81 = 544$.
3. (A) Let $\pi \colon \tilde X \to \mathbb{P}^3$ be the blow-up of the line $L = V_+(x_2, x_3)$, as in Example 1.5.18. Show that $[x_2 : x_3]$ defines a morphism $\tilde X \to \mathbb{P}^1$ whose fibres are the strict transforms of the planes through $L$, so that $H - E$ is the pullback of a point and $(H - E)^2 = 0$ as an intersection with any third divisor. Using $H^3 = 1$ and $H^2 \cdot E = 0$, deduce $H \cdot E^2 = -1$ and $E^3 = -2$, and then $(-K_{\tilde X})^3 = (4H - E)^3 = 54$.
4. (B) Let $f \colon X \to Y$ be the Atiyah contraction of Example 1.5.16, with $C = \operatorname{Exc}(f)$. On the first chart $C = V(x, w)$ and on the second $C = V(z, y)$. Using $z = xu$ and $y = wu$ on the overlap, show that the conormal sheaf of $C$ is $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(1)^{\oplus 2}$, so that $N_{C/X} \cong \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(-1)^{\oplus 2}$. Deduce $K_X \cdot C = 0$ from $\deg K_C = -2$ and the determinant of the normal bundle.
5. (A) Let $\pi_1 \colon X_1 \to \mathbb{A}^3$ be the blow-up of the origin, with exceptional divisor $E_1$, and $\pi_2 \colon X_2 \to X_1$ the blow-up of a point of $E_1$, with exceptional divisor $E_2$. Show that $\pi_2^* E_1 = E_1' + E_2$, where $E_1'$ is the strict transform, and that $K_{X_2/\mathbb{A}^3} = 2 E_1' + 4 E_2$. Check the coefficient $4$ by the Jacobian of the composite chart $x = a$, $y = ab$, $z = ac$, followed by $a = a$, $b = ab'$, $c = ac'$.
6. (B) Let $\pi_1 \colon X_1 \to \mathbb{A}^3$ be as in Problem 5. (a) Let $\pi_2$ be the blow-up of the strict transform of the $x$-axis, which meets $E_1$ in one point. Show that $K_{X_2/\mathbb{A}^3} = 2 E_1' + E_2$. (b) Let $\pi_2$ instead be the blow-up of a line contained in $E_1 \cong \mathbb{P}^2$. Show that $K_{X_2/\mathbb{A}^3} = 2 E_1' + 3 E_2$. In both cases check effectivity and support against Theorem 1.5.13.
7. (B) Let $Y_1 = V(xy - z^2) \subseteq \mathbb{A}^3$ and $Y_2 = V(xy - zw) \subseteq \mathbb{A}^4$, and let $\sigma_i$ be the blow-up of the ideal $(x, z)$ on $Y_i$. Show that the charts of $\operatorname{Bl}_{(x,z)} Y_1$ are $k[x, z/x]$ and $k[y, x/z]$, that $\operatorname{Exc}(\sigma_1)$ is a curve $\cong \mathbb{P}^1$ in a surface, and that $\operatorname{Bl}_{(x, z)} Y_2$ is the threefold $X$ of Example 1.5.16, with $\operatorname{Exc}(\sigma_2)$ a curve. Explain why Theorem 1.5.12 allows the first and forbids the second over a $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial base, using Example P1.8.22 and Example P1.8.23.
8. (B) In Example 1.5.11, let $\ell'$ be a fibre of $q \colon Y \to \mathbb{P}^{n-1}$, the strict transform of a line through $p$. Show that $\pi^* H \cdot \ell' = 1$, $E \cdot \ell' = 1$ and $K_Y \cdot \ell' = -2$. Show that the sections $x_1, \dots, x_n$ of $\mathcal{O}_Y(\pi^* H)$ all vanish on $E$ to order one, so that they define a morphism $Y \to \mathbb{P}^{n-1}$ given by sections of $\mathcal{O}_Y(\pi^* H - E)$. Show that it is constant on the fibres of $q$, and use Lemma 1.5.4 to show that it is $q$.
9. (B) Let $f \colon X \to \mathbb{P}^3$ be the blow-up of two distinct points $p_1, p_2$, with exceptional divisors $E_1, E_2$, and let $\tilde \ell$ be the strict transform of the line through $p_1$ and $p_2$. Show that $K_{X/\mathbb{P}^3} = 2E_1 + 2E_2$, that $E_i \cdot \tilde \ell = 1$ and $f^* H \cdot \tilde \ell = 1$, and that $K_X \cdot \tilde \ell = 0$. Find an effective exceptional $F$ with $F \cdot C < 0$ for every $f$-contracted curve, as in Corollary 1.5.24.
10. (C) Let $f \colon X \to Y$ be the Atiyah contraction and $g \colon Z \to X$ the blow-up of $C$. Using Proposition 1.5.14 and Problem 4, show that $Z$ is smooth with exceptional divisor $G \cong \mathbb{P}(N_{C/X}) \cong \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$. On the chart $x = x$, $w = x w'$ of $Z$ over the first chart of $X$, write $f \circ g$ in coordinates and identify it with the chart of $Y'$ in Example 1.5.17, so that $Z \cong Y'$ over $Y$. Since $f$ has no exceptional divisor, $K_X = f^* K_Y$ for the Cartier divisor $K_Y$ of the hypersurface $Y$, in agreement with $K_X \cdot C = 0$ in Problem 4; deduce that $K_{Y'} = \sigma^* K_Y + G$.
