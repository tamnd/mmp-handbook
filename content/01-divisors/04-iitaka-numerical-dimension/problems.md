## Problems

Each problem is a computation with the tools of this chapter: the semigroup and the stable field of Section 1, the projection formula and products of Section 2, and the counts of $h^0(mD + A)$ of Section 3.

1. (A) On $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$, let $D$ have bidegree $(a, b)$. Compute $h^0(mD)$, $N(D)$ and $e(D)$ for all integers $a, b$, and find the constants $a_0, b_0$ of Theorem 1.4.6 for $(a, b) = (2, 0)$ and $(a, b) = (1, 3)$. Show that $\kappa_\sigma(D) = \kappa(D)$ for every $D$ on $X$.
2. (A) Let $E$ be an elliptic curve with origin $O$, $p \in E$ a point of order 6 and $D = p - O$. Show that $h^0(mD) = 1$ if $6 \mid m$ and $0$ otherwise. Compute $N(D)$, $e(D)$, $\kappa(D)$ and $\kappa_\sigma(D)$, and the field $k(\varphi_m)$ for $m \in N(D)$.
3. (A) Let $X = \operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2$ with $\ell$ and $E$ as in Example 1.4.10. Compute $\kappa$ and $\kappa_\sigma$ of $E$, $\ell - E$, $\ell$, $2\ell - E$ and $\ell - 2E$. For $\ell - 2E$, show that $(\ell - 2E) \cdot (\ell - E) < 0$ while $\ell - E$ is nef, and deduce that $\ell - 2E$ is not pseudo-effective.
4. (B) Let $F_n$ be the Hirzebruch surface with negative section $C_-$, $C_-^2 = -n$, $n \ge 1$, and fibre $F$. Show that $h^0(mC_-) = 1$ for all $m \ge 0$. For the ample divisor $A = C_- + (n + 1)F$, show that $(mC_- + A) \cdot C_- < 0$ for $m \ge 2$, that $C_-$ is then a fixed component of $|mC_- + A|$, and that $h^0(mC_- + A) = h^0(C_- + A)$ for all $m \ge 1$. Conclude that $\kappa(C_-) = \kappa_\sigma(C_-) = 0$.
5. (B) Let $E$ be an elliptic curve, $S = E \times E$, and $\Gamma_n$ the graph of multiplication by $n \ge 2$. Show that $\Gamma_n \cdot f_1 = 1$, $\Gamma_n \cdot f_2 = n^2$, $\Gamma_n \cdot \Delta = (n - 1)^2$ and $\Gamma_n^2 = 0$, with the notation of Example 1.4.19. Show that the class $(n^2 - n) f_1 + (1 - n) f_2 + n\Delta$ has the same intersection numbers with $f_1$, $f_2$ and $\Delta$ and has square $0$. Use the automorphism $(x, y) \mapsto (x, y - nx)$ to show that $\kappa(\Gamma_n) = \kappa_\sigma(\Gamma_n) = 1$, and describe the Iitaka fibration of $\Gamma_n$.
6. (A) Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^3$ be a smooth surface of degree $d$. Compute $\kappa(X)$ for every $d \ge 1$, and compute $P_1(X)$ and $P_2(X)$ for $d = 4, 5, 6$.
7. (A) Let $C_g$ denote a smooth projective curve of genus $g$. Compute $\kappa$ of $C_0 \times C_3$, $C_1 \times C_1 \times C_5$ and $C_2 \times C_2 \times C_2$, and compute $P_2(C_1 \times C_2)$ and $P_2(C_2 \times C_3)$ with Proposition P5.11.5.
8. (B) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ in the nine base points of the pencil spanned by the cubics $x^3 + y^3 + z^3$ and $xyz$, which are distinct by Example P5.10.27. Show that $-K_X$ is the class of a fibre of the morphism $X \to \mathbb{P}^1$ given by the pencil, that $h^0(-mK_X) = m + 1$, and that $\kappa(-K_X) = \kappa_\sigma(-K_X) = 1$. Contrast this with the nine points of Example P5.10.26, where $\kappa(-K) = 0 < 1 = \kappa_\sigma(-K)$.
9. (B) Let $E$ be an elliptic curve, $P$ of degree 0 and order exactly $r \ge 1$, $X = \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{O}_E \oplus P)$ and $C_0$ as in Theorem 1.4.26. Show that $h^0(mC_0) = \lfloor m/r \rfloor + 1$, that $N(C_0) = \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ and $e(C_0) = 1$, and that $Y_m$ is a rational normal curve of degree $\lfloor m/r \rfloor$ for $m \ge r$. Compute $h^0(mC_0 + cA)$ for $A = C_0 + f$, and check that $\kappa(C_0) = \kappa_\sigma(C_0) = 1$.
10. (B) In the surface of Theorem 1.4.26, with $P$ of infinite order, let $D = 2C_0 + f$. Compute $D^2$, show that $D$ is ample, and compute $h^0(mD)$ for all $m \ge 1$ by pushing forward to $E$. Check that it grows like $m^2$, and that $\kappa(C_0) = 0$ while $\kappa(C_0 + \varepsilon D) = 2$ for every rational $\varepsilon > 0$.
11. (C) Let $g \colon X' \to X$ be a finite surjective morphism of normal projective varieties of degree $d$, and $D$ a Cartier divisor on $X$. Using the norm map, which sends a section $s$ of $g^*\mathcal{O}_X(mD)$ to a section $\operatorname{Nm}(s)$ of $\mathcal{O}_X(dmD)$, show that every ratio $s/t$ of two sections of $g^*\mathcal{O}_X(mD)$ is a root of the polynomial $\operatorname{Nm}(\lambda t - s)/\operatorname{Nm}(t)$, whose coefficients lie in $k(\varphi_{dm})$. Deduce that $K_\infty(g^*D)$ is algebraic over $K_\infty(D)$ and that $\kappa(X', g^*D) = \kappa(X, D)$. With Proposition 1.4.14 and the Stein factorization, extend this to a surjective morphism that is generically finite.
