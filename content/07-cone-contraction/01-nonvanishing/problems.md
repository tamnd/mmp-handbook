## Problems

Each problem computes a threshold on an explicit resolution, tests a hypothesis of Theorem 7.1.7 on an example, or runs a step of its proof in low dimension.

1. (A) Let $M = \{ y^2 = x^5 \}$ near the origin of $\mathbb{A}^2$. Find a log resolution, list the pairs $(a_j, r_j)$ of discrepancy and multiplicity, and show that $c_0 = 7/10$, attained along a single exceptional curve.
2. (A) Let $M = \{ xyz = 0 \}$ in $\mathbb{A}^3$. Show that the identity and the blow-up of the origin are both log resolutions, that the blow-up has $a = 2$ and $r = 3$, and that $c_0 = 1$ is attained along four divisors on the blow-up. Break the tie with Lemma 7.1.3.
3. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ and $D$ a divisor of bidegree $(1, 0)$. Check that $D$ is nef, that $D - K_X$ is ample, and compute $h^0(X, mD)$ for all $m \ge 0$.
4. (A) Let $X$ be a smooth projective curve of genus $2$ and $D = p - q$ for distinct points $p, q$ with $p - q$ not torsion. Show that $D$ is nef, that no $aD + G - K_X$ with $G = 0$ is big, and that $h^0(mD) = 0$ for all $m \ge 1$.
5. (B) Let $X$ be smooth projective of dimension $n \ge 2$, $A$ ample and $x \in X$. Show that for every $\varepsilon > 0$ and $q$ divisible enough there is $M \in |qA|$ with $\operatorname{mult}_x M \ge q \bigl( (A^n)^{1/n} - \varepsilon \bigr)$. Explain why Step 2 of the proof of Theorem 7.1.7 needs $(L^n) > n^n$.
6. (B) In Step 3 of the proof, take $X = \mathbb{P}^2$, $A$ a line, $q = 1$ and $M$ a union of three concurrent lines through $x$, so $\operatorname{mult}_x M = 3 > nq = 2$. Compute $c_0$ on the blow-up of $x$ and check $c_0 < 1/q$.
7. (B) Let $X$ be a smooth projective surface, $D$ nef with $D \not\equiv 0$ and $D^2 = 0$, and $A$ ample. Show that $((eD + A)^2) = (A^2) + 2e (D \cdot A)$, and find the least $e$ for which Step 2 of the proof applies.
8. (B) Show that Theorem 7.1.7 fails without the condition $\lceil G \rceil \ge 0$: on $X = \mathbb{P}^1$ take $D = 0$ and $G = -p$ for a point $p$, and check that $aD + G - K_X$ is ample while $H^0(X, mD + \lceil G \rceil) = 0$.
9. (B) Let $(X, \Delta)$ be a projective klt pair with $-(K_X + \Delta)$ nef and big. Show that $H^i(X, \mathcal{O}_X) = 0$ for $i > 0$, and deduce that $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$ is finitely generated with no nonzero divisor class algebraically equivalent to $0$.
10. (C) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at nine general points, with $-K_X$ nef and $(K_X^2) = 0$. Show that $D = -K_X$ satisfies the hypothesis of Theorem 7.1.7 with $G = 0$ for no $a$, and that $h^0(-mK_X) = 1$ for all $m \ge 0$, so $D$ is not semiample.
