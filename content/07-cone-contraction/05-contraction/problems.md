## Problems

Each problem computes a contraction, its type and the induced data on the target, or tests a step of the proof of Theorem 7.5.2 on an example.

1. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ and $\Delta = \frac12 D$ for the diagonal $D$. Find the contractions of the two rays and of the whole cone, and compute $\Delta_Y = \varphi_* \Delta$ for each.
2. (A) Let $X = \operatorname{Bl}_{p} \mathbb{P}^2 = \mathbb{F}_1$. Compute the exact sequence of Corollary 7.5.3 for both rays.
3. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$. For each of the six nonzero proper faces of $\overline{NE}(X)$, give the contraction and verify $\rho(Y) = \rho(X) - \dim \operatorname{span} F$.
4. (B) Let $X = \operatorname{Bl}_{p_1, p_2} \mathbb{P}^2$ with the notation of Proposition P7.4.20. List all $K_X$-negative extremal faces, their supporting divisors and their contractions, and decide the type of each contraction of a ray.
5. (B) Let $X = \operatorname{Bl}_C \mathbb{P}^3$ for a smooth conic $C$. Show that $\rho(X) = 2$, that the strict transform of the plane of $C$ is a divisor $P \cong \mathbb{P}^2$ with $\mathcal{O}_P(P) = \mathcal{O}(-1)$, and find the two extremal rays, their lengths and their contractions.
6. (B) Let $\mathcal{E} = \mathcal{O} \oplus \mathcal{O}(1)$ on $\mathbb{P}^2$ and $X = \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E})$. Identify $X$ with $\operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^3$, compute $K_X$ in terms of $\xi$ and $h$, and find the two contractions with their types.
7. (B) Let $\varphi$ be the divisorial contraction of $\mathbb{F}_n$ with $\Delta = c \, C_-$ onto $\mathbb{P}(1, 1, n)$ of Example 7.5.15. Compute the index of $K_Y$ and check the $\mathbb{Q}$-factoriality of $Y$ directly, by showing that $n \ell$ is Cartier for the image $\ell$ of a fibre.
8. (B) For the small contraction of Example 7.5.14, show that the Weil divisor $\varphi(\pi^{-1}(L))$, for a line $L \subseteq \mathbb{P}^2$, has no Cartier multiple at the vertex, by restricting to the image of the section $S$ and its complement.
9. (B) Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^2 \times \mathbb{P}^2$ be a smooth divisor of bidegree $(2, 2)$. Compute $K_X$, show that $X$ is Fano with $\rho(X) = 2$, and show that both projections are conic bundles.
10. (C) Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^3$ be a smooth divisor of bidegree $(1, 2)$. Show that $p_2$ is the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^3$ along the intersection of two quadrics, a curve of genus $1$, and that $p_1$ is a quadric surface fibration, and compute the lengths of both rays.
11. (C) Let $(X, \Delta)$ be a $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial klt pair and $\varphi$ a divisorial contraction with exceptional divisor $E$. Show that if $\varphi(E)$ is a point, then $E \cdot C < 0$ for every curve $C \subseteq E$ and $-E|_E$ is ample.
