## Problems

Each problem computes a nef threshold and its nef value morphism on an explicit pair, or runs a step of the proof of Theorem 7.3.4 by hand.

1. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ and $H$ of tridegree $(1, 2, 3)$. Compute $r(H)$ and identify the nef value morphism.
2. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^2$ and $H$ of bidegree $(p, q)$ with $p, q > 0$. Compute $r(H)$, and show that the nef value morphism is a projection or the map to a point according as $3p - 2q$ is nonzero or zero.
3. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{F}_n$ with negative section $\sigma$, $\sigma^2 = -n$, and fibre $f$, so $K_X = -2\sigma - (n + 2) f$. For $H = \sigma + (n + 1) f$, compute $r(H)$ and show that the nef value morphism is the ruling $X \to \mathbb{P}^1$.
4. (A) Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^{n+1}$ be a smooth hypersurface of degree $d \le n + 1$ and $H$ a hyperplane section. Compute $r(H)$ and check the bound of Theorem 7.3.4.
5. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}(1, 1, 2)$, with $\mathcal{O}_X(2)$ Cartier and $K_X = \mathcal{O}_X(-4)$ as a Weil divisor class. Take $\Delta = (1/2) C$ for a general member $C \in |\mathcal{O}_X(2)|$, find the least $a$ with $a(K_X + \Delta)$ Cartier, and compute $r(\mathcal{O}_X(2))$.
6. (B) In Example 7.3.9 with $H = xh - ye$, $x > y > 0$, determine the set of $(x, y)$ for which $r(H)$ is attained on both extremal rays at once, and describe the nef value morphism there.
7. (B) For $X = \mathbb{P}^2$, $\Delta = 0$, $a = 1$ and $H$ a line, write down the strip of Step 1 of the proof, the polynomial $P(p, q) = h^0(\mathcal{O}(p - 3q))$ on $R_0$, and the nonzero polynomial of degree $2$ that vanishes on the strip. Explain why the proof produces no contradiction.
8. (B) Let $(X, \Delta)$ be klt with $K_X + \Delta$ not nef and $H_1, H_2$ ample. Show that $r(H_1 + H_2) \ge \min\{ r(H_1), r(H_2) \}$, and give an example on $\operatorname{Bl}_x \mathbb{P}^2$ where the inequality is strict.
9. (B) Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety with $K_X$ not nef and $H$ ample. Show that the nef value morphism of $H$ is birational if $H + r(H) K_X$ is big, and that every contracted curve $C$ then satisfies $-K_X \cdot C \le n + 1$ when $H \cdot C = 1$.
10. (C) Let $(X, \Delta)$ be klt of dimension $n$ and $H$ ample. Show that if the nef value morphism of $H$ maps $X$ to a point, then $-(K_X + \Delta) \equiv H / r(H)$, so $-(K_X + \Delta)$ is ample. Show that for $X = \mathbb{P}^n$, $\Delta = 0$ and $H = \mathcal{O}(d)$ this always happens, with $r(H) = d/(n + 1)$.
11. (C) Prove the relative rationality theorem for $\pi \colon X \to S$ projective, KM98, Theorem 3.24: if $K_X + \Delta$ is not $\pi$-nef and $H$ is $\pi$-ample, the $\pi$-nef threshold is $u/v$ with $v \le a(n + 1)$. Identify which steps of the proof of Theorem 7.3.4 need the relative forms of vanishing and of Theorem 7.2.1.
