# The base point free theorem

This chapter proves the base point free theorem of Kawamata and Shokurov: on a projective klt pair $(X, \Delta)$, a nef Cartier divisor $D$ such that $aD - (K_X + \Delta)$ is nef and big for some $a > 0$ has $|bD|$ free for every $b \gg 0$. It is the theorem that turns a supporting divisor of an extremal face into a morphism, and Chapter 5 uses it in exactly that way. The ground field is algebraically closed of characteristic $0$.

Section 1 proves the theorem, following KM98, Theorem 3.3. Nonvanishing, Corollary 7.1.8, gives sections of $mD$ for $m \gg 0$. If $|mD|$ has base points, a log resolution of its base ideal, the tie-breaking lemma and Kawamata-Viehweg vanishing produce sections of $b'D$ for all $b' \gg 0$ that do not vanish on the image of the chosen divisor $F_0$, which lies in $\operatorname{Bs} |mD|$. So the base loci of $|p^u D|$ decrease strictly until they are empty, for every prime $p$, and two primes give every large $b$. The examples are the two contractions of the plane blown up at a point and Zariski's nef and big divisor that is not semiample, where the hypothesis fails.

Section 2 draws the consequences. The divisor $D$ is the pullback of an ample divisor by a contraction $\varphi \colon X \to Z$, which contracts exactly the curves $C$ with $D \cdot C = 0$. A nef and big $K_X + \Delta$ on a klt pair is semiample. On a klt pair with $-(K_X + \Delta)$ nef and big every nef divisor is semiample and numerical equivalence of Cartier divisors is linear equivalence. The example is the plane blown up at three collinear points, whose anticanonical model has an $A_1$ point.
