## Problems

Each problem checks the hypothesis of Theorem 7.2.1 on an explicit variety, computes the contraction of Corollary 7.2.4, or runs a step of the proof by hand.

1. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{F}_n$ be the Hirzebruch surface with negative section $\sigma$, $\sigma^2 = -n$, and fibre $f$, $n \ge 1$. Show that $D = \sigma + nf$ is nef and big with $D \cdot \sigma = 0$, and that $aD - K_X$ is nef for some $a > 0$ exactly when $n \le 2$. For $n \le 2$ identify the contraction of Corollary 7.2.4 as the contraction of $\sigma$ to the vertex of the cone over a rational normal curve of degree $n$.
2. (A) For $n \ge 3$ in Problem 1, find the rational $t < 1$ for which $aD - (K_X + t\sigma)$ is nef and big for large $a$, so that Theorem 7.2.1 applies to the klt pair $(X, t\sigma)$. Compare the least such $t$ with the discrepancy $-1 + 2/n$ of $\sigma$ over the vertex.
3. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ and $D$ of tridegree $(1, 1, 0)$. Verify the hypothesis of Theorem 7.2.1 and find the contraction.
4. (A) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at two points, with exceptional curves $E_1, E_2$ and $\ell'$ the strict transform of the line through them. Find all nef divisors $D$ with $D \cdot C = 0$ for exactly one of the three curves $E_1, E_2, \ell'$, and the corresponding contractions.
5. (B) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at six points, five of them on a smooth conic $Q$ and one off it, with no three collinear. Show that $-K_X$ is ample, and find a nef divisor whose contraction maps the strict transform of $Q$ to a point and contracts no other curve.
6. (B) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at four points on a line. Show that $-K_X$ is not nef, and find the curve $C$ with $K_X \cdot C > 0$.
7. (B) In Step 1 of the proof of Theorem 7.2.1 take $X = \mathbb{P}^2$, $\Delta = 0$, $D$ a line and $m = 1$, but replace $|mD|$ by the subsystem of lines through a point $x$. Compute $F_{\mathfrak{b}}$, $c_0$ and $F_0$ on the blow-up of $x$, and check that $f(F_0) = x$ is not a base point of $|b'D|$.
8. (B) Let $F$ be a smooth Fano variety, $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times F$ and $D = \operatorname{pr}_1^* \mathcal{O}(1)$. Show that $aD - K_X$ is ample for every $a \ge 0$, that the contraction of Corollary 7.2.4 is $\operatorname{pr}_1$, and that $D \cdot C = 0$ exactly for the curves in the fibres.
9. (C) Let $X$ be a smooth projective surface with $K_X$ nef and big. Show that the contraction of Corollary 7.2.5 contracts exactly the $(-2)$-curves, so that its image is the canonical model with rational double points.
10. (C) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at three collinear points of Example 7.2.7. Show that $-K_X$ is nef and big, so every nef divisor is semiample by Corollary 7.2.6. Find the extremal rays of the nef cone, and for each face describe the contraction given by Corollary 7.2.4.
