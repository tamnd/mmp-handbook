## Problems

Each problem computes a cone, a length, a contraction or a cohomology group for an lc pair, or compares the two proofs of the cone theorem on an example.

1. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^2$ and $\Delta = \ell_1 + \ell_2 + c \, \ell_3$ for three lines in general position and rational $0 \le c \le 1$. Decide when $(X, \Delta)$ is lc and when it is klt, and compute the length of the ray.
2. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ and $\Delta = D_1 + D_2$ for a fibre $D_1$ of the first projection and a fibre $D_2$ of the second. Find the $K_\Delta$-negative rays, their lengths and their contractions.
3. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^3$ and $\Delta$ the union of four general planes. Show that $K_X + \Delta \sim 0$, list the lc centres by dimension, and compute $h^1(\mathcal{O}_\Delta)$ and $h^2(\mathcal{O}_\Delta)$.
4. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{F}_n$ and $\Delta = C_- + c \, F_0$ for a fibre $F_0$ and rational $0 \le c \le 1$. Show that the hypothesis of Theorem 7.6.2 holds, compute the two lengths, and find the contraction of each ray together with $\Delta_Y$.
5. (B) For the cone $X$ over a smooth plane cubic, compute $\rho(X)$ by the blow-up $Y \to X$ of the vertex, and show that $\operatorname{Cl}(X) / \operatorname{Pic}(X)$ is infinite.
6. (B) Let $X$ be the cone over a smooth plane conic and $\Delta = \ell_1 + \ell_2$ for two distinct lines through the vertex. Show that $(X, \Delta)$ is lc, compute $K_X + \Delta$, and decide whether $X$ has a $K_\Delta$-negative ray.
7. (B) Let $(X, \Delta) = (\mathbb{P}^2, Q)$ for a smooth conic $Q$. Use Theorem 7.6.8 to show that $H^0(\mathcal{O}(m)) \to H^0(\mathcal{O}_Q(m))$ is surjective for $m \ge 0$, and check it by computing dimensions.
8. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^2$. For $\Delta = 0$, compare the descriptions of the cone given by Theorem 6.2.9 and Theorem 7.4.6, and check Proposition 7.6.16 for both rays.
9. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}(1, 1, n)$ and $\Delta = c \, \ell_0$ for a line $\ell_0$ through the vertex and rational $0 \le c \le 1$. Show that $(X, \Delta)$ is lc for $c \le 1$, klt for $c < 1$, and compute the length of the ray as a function of $c$ and $n$.
10. (C) Let $X$ be the cone over a smooth curve $E$ of degree $3$ in $\mathbb{P}^2$, and $\mu \colon Y \to X$ the blow-up of the vertex. Using the Leray spectral sequence, show that $H^1(Y, \mathcal{O}_Y) \cong H^0(X, R^1 \mu_* \mathcal{O}_Y)$, compute both, and explain why this does not contradict Theorem 7.6.8 on $X$.
11. (C) Let $(X, \Delta)$ be lc with a klt boundary $\Delta_0$, and $R$ a $K_\Delta$-negative ray. Show that $R$ is $K_{\Delta_t}$-negative for all small $t > 0$, and give an example on $\mathbb{F}_n$ where a $K_{\Delta_t}$-negative ray for all small $t > 0$ is $K_\Delta$-trivial, so that it disappears in the limit.
