# Log canonical pairs and the two proofs compared

This chapter extends the cone and contraction theorems from klt pairs to lc pairs and compares the proof of this volume with Mori's proof of Volume 6. The extension comes in two steps of very different depth. The first is a perturbation argument that uses nothing beyond Chapters 4 and 5. The second, which needs new vanishing theorems, is stated with sketches of proofs only.

Section 1 treats lc pairs $(X, \Delta)$ for which $X$ carries some klt boundary, for example lc pairs on klt or smooth varieties. Moving $\Delta$ slightly towards the klt boundary gives klt pairs whose canonical classes converge to $K_X + \Delta$, and the cone theorem, the length bound $2 \dim X$ and the contraction theorem pass to the limit; the length bound uses that the degrees lie in a discrete set. The cone over a plane cubic shows that not every lc pair is of this kind.

Section 2 states the theorems of Ambro and Fujino for arbitrary lc pairs: Fujino's vanishing and torsion freeness theorems, the base point free theorem, and the cone and contraction theorems. The proofs are sketched and marked as sketches. They treat the non-klt locus as a lower dimensional object with its own quasi-log structure and lift sections from it.

Section 3 compares the two proofs of the cone theorem: Mori's, from rational curves, in every characteristic, for smooth varieties, with the bound $\dim X + 1$; and the klt proof, from nef divisors and vanishing, in characteristic $0$, for klt pairs, with the bound $2 \dim X$ and with contractions. The examples $\mathbb{P}(1, 1, n)$ and $\mathbb{F}_n$ with a boundary show what only the second proof sees, and positive characteristic shows what only the first proof reaches.
