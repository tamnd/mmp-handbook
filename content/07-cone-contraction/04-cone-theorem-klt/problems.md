## Problems

Each problem computes a cone, a threshold or a length for a klt pair, or tests a step of the proof of Theorem 7.4.6 on an example.

1. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ and $\Delta = c_1 F_1 + c_2 F_2$ for fibres $F_1$, $F_2$ of the two rulings and rational $0 \le c_1, c_2 < 1$. Compute the lengths of the two rays and the threshold $r(H)$ for $H = (1, 1)$.
2. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^3$ and $\Delta = \frac12 (H_1 + H_2 + H_3 + H_4)$ for four general planes. Show that $(X, \Delta)$ is klt, that $K_X + \Delta \equiv -2 H$, and compute $r(\mathcal{O}(1))$ and its denominator bound.
3. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}(1, 1, 2)$, the quadric cone. Compute the index $a$ of $K_X$, the length of the ray, and the threshold $r(\mathcal{O}_X(2))$, where $\mathcal{O}_X(2)$ is the hyperplane class of the embedding as a cone in $\mathbb{P}^3$.
4. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^2$ with the notation of Example 7.4.10. For $H = (\alpha, \beta)$ ample, compute $r(H)$ and the face cut out by $H + r(H) K_X$, and find the $H$ for which this face is the whole cone.
5. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{F}_n$, $n \ge 1$, and $\Delta = c \, C_-$ with rational $0 \le c < 1$. Find the set of $c$ for which $\overline{NE}(X)_{K_\Delta \ge 0} \ne 0$, and for each $c$ decide which of the two rays of Proposition P7.4.19 is $K_\Delta$-negative.
6. (B) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at $r \le 8$ general points and $\Delta = c E_1$ for an exceptional curve $E_1$. Compute $K_\Delta \cdot C$ for every $(-1)$-curve $C$, and find the largest $c < 1$ for which $(X, \Delta)$ is log del Pezzo.
7. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ and $\Delta = \frac12 D$ for a smooth divisor $D$ of class $(1, 1, 1)$. Show that $X$ with $\Delta$ is log Fano, list the extremal faces of all dimensions with their supporting divisors, and compare with Corollary 7.4.7.
8. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}(1, 1, n)$ as in Example 7.4.21 and $\Delta = c \, \ell$ for a line $\ell$ through the vertex. Find the $c$ for which $(X, \Delta)$ is klt, using the pullback of $\ell$ to $\mathbb{F}_n$, and compute the length of the ray.
9. (B) Let $f \colon X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \to S = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ be a projection. Compute $\overline{NE}(X/S)$ and its $K_X$-negative rays, and check Proposition 7.4.15.
10. (C) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at the nine base points of a general pencil of cubics and $\Delta = c F$ for a smooth fibre $F$ and rational $0 < c < 1$. Show that $K_\Delta \equiv -(1 - c) F$, that every $(-1)$-curve spans a $K_\Delta$-negative ray of length $1 - c$, and that for an ample $H$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ only finitely many of them are $(K_\Delta + \varepsilon H)$-negative, with an explicit bound on $H \cdot E$ in terms of $\varepsilon$ and $c$.
11. (C) Let $(X, \Delta)$ be klt and $R$ a $K_\Delta$-negative extremal ray with supporting divisor $L$ from Corollary 7.4.7. Show that for $X = \mathbb{F}_n$ with $\Delta = c \, C_-$ as in Example 7.4.22, the set of all nef $L$ with $F_L = R$ is the open ray of $F$ or of $C_+$, and that every such $L$ satisfies $L - K_\Delta$ ample after scaling.
