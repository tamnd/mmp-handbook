# Finite generation

The theorem of Birkar, Cascini, Hacon and McKernan has as its most quoted consequence the finite generation of the canonical ring of a smooth projective variety, and more generally of the log canonical ring of a klt pair. This chapter derives finite generation from the existence of log terminal models, works it out by hand for surfaces, and collects the corollaries that are used in the rest of the handbook.

The first section proves that the log canonical ring of a klt pair with big log canonical divisor is finitely generated, from a log terminal model, the base point free theorem of Volume 7 and a lemma on Veronese subrings that is proved in full. The case of a log canonical divisor that is not big is reduced to the big case by the canonical bundle formula of Fujino and Mori, which is cited. Adjoint rings, graded by several boundaries at once, are treated at the level of statements.

The second section does the surface case without BCHM: Zariski's computation of the fixed part of $|mD|$, the minimal model program for klt surfaces of Volume 2 and log abundance for surfaces give finite generation of every klt log canonical ring of a surface. Two rings are computed in closed form, and Zariski's example of a nef and big divisor with non-finitely generated section ring shows that the adjoint form of the divisor matters.

The third section draws the corollaries: log canonical models, the existence of klt flips, and $\mathbb{Q}$-factorializations of klt varieties, with the cone over a quadric surface as the worked example.
