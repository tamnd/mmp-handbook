## Problems

Computations of log canonical rings, Zariski decompositions and the corollaries of finite generation.

1. (B) Let $L_1, \ldots, L_7 \subseteq \mathbb{P}^2$ be lines in general position and $\Delta = \frac{1}{2} \sum L_i$. Compute $\deg \lfloor m(K + \Delta) \rfloor$ for every $m$, realise $R(\mathbb{P}^2, K + \Delta)$ as a subring of $S[w]/(w^2 - h)$, with $h$ the product of the seven lines, graded by $\deg x_i = 2$ and $\deg w = 7$, and find its generators.
2. (B) Let $C$ be a smooth projective curve of genus $2$. Show that $R(C, K_C) \cong k[x, y, z]/(z^2 - f(x, y))$ with $\deg x = \deg y = 1$, $\deg z = 3$ and $f$ a binary sextic, by comparing $h^0(C, mK_C)$ with the Hilbert function of the quotient.
3. (B) Let $S$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at three collinear points, with $\ell'$ the strict transform of the line, and $D = \pi^* 2L + \ell'$. Compute the Zariski decomposition of $D$ and $R(S, D)^{(m)}$ for a suitable $m$.
4. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}(1, 1, 2)$ and $\Delta = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i=1}^{5} B_i$ with $B_i$ general members of $|\mathcal{O}(2)|$. Show that $(X, \Delta)$ is klt, compute $\lfloor m(K_X + \Delta) \rfloor$, and write down $R(X, K_X + \Delta)$.
5. (B) Let $S_0$ be a smooth surface of degree $d \ge 5$ in $\mathbb{P}^3$ and $S$ its blow-up at $r$ points. Compute $R(S, K_S)$ and its Hilbert series.
6. (B) For the cone $X$ over a smooth quadric surface in Example 9.5.17, compute $a(E, X_1', 0)$ for the exceptional divisor $E$ of the blow-up $W \to X_1'$ along $C$, and verify that $X_1'$ and $X_2'$ are both log terminal models of $(W, (1 - \varepsilon) E)$ over $X$.
7. (C) Let $X$ be the cone over a smooth cubic surface $T \subseteq \mathbb{P}^3$, embedded by $-K_T$. Show that the discrepancy of the exceptional divisor of the blow-up of the vertex is $0$, that $X$ is not $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial, and describe the $\mathbb{Q}$-factorializations produced by Corollary 9.5.16 in terms of contractions of $(-1)$-curves on $T$.
8. (C) Let $S$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at nine general points of a smooth cubic, $C$ the strict transform of the cubic, and $D = \pi^* 3L - \sum E_i + C$. Show that $D$ is not semiample and that its Zariski decomposition has $N = 0$, and decide whether $R(S, D)$ is finitely generated.
