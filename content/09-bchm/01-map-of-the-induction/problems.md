## Problems

Each problem places a small example on the map of the induction or runs a program with scaling by hand.

1. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ with fibres $F_1, F_2$ and let $V$ be the span of two general fibres $G_1, G_1' \sim F_1$ and two general fibres $G_2, G_2' \sim F_2$. With $A = \varepsilon (F_1 + F_2)$ for a small rational $\varepsilon > 0$, describe the set $L_A(V)$ and the decomposition of $E_A(V)$ into the sets of divisors with a common lc model, and check the statement $E_2$ of Theorem 9.1.8 on this example.
2. (B) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at two points, with exceptional curves $E_1, E_2$ and line class $L$. For $H = aL - bE_1 - cE_2$ ample, find the conditions on $a, b, c$ under which the $K_X$-program with scaling of $H$ first contracts $E_1$, first contracts $E_2$, or first contracts the strict transform of the line through the two points.
3. (B) Prove Lemma 9.1.13 in the case of a birational morphism $f \colon X \to Y$ of smooth projective surfaces that blows down a single $(-1)$-curve, by a direct computation with the pullback of $K_Y + f_* \Delta$.
4. (B) For the weighted blow-up $X \to \mathbb{P}^3$ with weights $(1, 2, 3)$ of Section 2, compute the stable base locus of every class in the interior of the effective cone of $X$, and check that the chambers of Example 9.1.21 are the sets of classes with a common stable base locus and a common ample model.
5. (B) Let $X$ be a smooth projective curve of genus $g \ge 2$ and $\Delta = \sum_i b_i p_i$ a boundary with coefficients in $[0, 1)$. Show directly that $R(X, K_X + \Delta)$ is finitely generated, and bound the degrees of a set of generators in terms of $g$ and the denominators of the $b_i$.
6. (C) Run the toric $K$-program with scaling of a general ample divisor on the toric variety $X^+$ of Section 2 after a further star subdivision at $w = e_1 + e_2 + e_3$, and determine how many distinct sequences of models occur as the ample divisor varies.
7. (C) Give an example of a projective $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial klt surface pair $(X, \Delta)$ with $K_X + \Delta$ pseudo-effective but not big, and a program with scaling of an ample divisor that ends with a log terminal model, and compute its thresholds. Explain which of the statements $A_2$ to $F_2$ the example uses and which it does not need.
8. (C) Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety with $K_X$ big. Write $K_X \sim_{\mathbb{Q}} A + B$ with $A$ ample and $B \ge 0$, and show that for a general $A' \sim_{\mathbb{Q}} A$ and small rational $\varepsilon > 0$ the pair $(X, \varepsilon(A' + B))$ is klt with big boundary and $K_X + \varepsilon(A' + B) \sim_{\mathbb{Q}} (1 + \varepsilon) K_X$. Deduce the finite generation of the canonical ring of $X$ from Theorem 9.1.3, and explain why the same argument needs more for $K_X$ pseudo-effective but not big.
