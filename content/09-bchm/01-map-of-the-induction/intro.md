# The map of the induction

This chapter is a printed map of the proof of BCHM10. The theorem of Birkar, Cascini, Hacon and McKernan is proved by an induction on the dimension that runs through six statements at once, and the rest of the volume is organised by the arrows of that induction. The chapter states the six statements and the main theorem precisely, proves what is formal about them, and works one example in full in which the program that the induction runs, the program with scaling, can be computed by hand.

Section 1 sets up the language of models: birational contractions, weak log canonical models, log terminal models and log canonical models, and the sets $\mathcal{L}_A(V)$ of boundaries with a general ample part. It states the main theorem of BCHM10 and the six theorems $A$ to $F$: the existence of pl flips, special finiteness, the existence of log terminal models, nonvanishing, the finiteness of models and finite generation. It states the six implications from dimension $n - 1$ to dimension $n$ and says where in the volume each is proved. The formal parts are proved in full: the statements in dimension $1$, the induction itself, the comparison of sections on a weak log canonical model, and the deduction of the main theorem from the six statements.

Section 2 is the worked example. On a toric variety every step of the program is a computation with fans, and the thresholds of the program with scaling are ratios of intersection numbers with finitely many wall curves. The example is the flip $X^+$ of the weighted blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^3$ with weights $(1, 2, 3)$ from Volume 3. For the scaling divisor $3D_0 + 2D_3$ the program is a flip, a divisorial contraction to $\mathbb{P}^3$ and the fibration to a point, with thresholds $1 > \frac56 > \frac45$; for $D_0 + D_3$ it is a divisorial contraction to $\mathbb{P}(1, 1, 2, 3)$ and the fibration to a point, with thresholds $3 > \frac73$. The two programs are two walks through the chambers of the secondary fan, and the line through $-K_{X^+}$ separates the two choices.
