# BCHM: existence of minimal models

This volume proves the theorem of Birkar, Cascini, Hacon and McKernan (BCHM10): a klt pair with a big boundary and a pseudo-effective log canonical divisor has a log terminal model, and log canonical rings of klt pairs are finitely generated. Chapters 1 to 3 set up the induction, pl flips and the program with scaling. Chapter 4 proves the finiteness of models and outlines nonvanishing. Chapter 5 derives finite generation and its corollaries, and Chapter 6 treats Mori dream spaces.
