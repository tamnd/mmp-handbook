## Problems

Each problem runs a program with scaling, tests a hypothesis of the termination theorem, or follows lc centres through a sequence of flips.

1. (B) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at three non-collinear points. For an ample $H = aL - b_1 E_1 - b_2 E_2 - b_3 E_3$, describe the $K$-program with scaling of $H$ and the regions of $(a, b_1, b_2, b_3)$ that lead to each of its possible outcomes.
2. (B) Run the $(K_X + \Delta)$-program with scaling of an ample divisor on $X = \operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2$ with $\Delta = \frac12 G$ for a general $G \in |4L - E|$, decide whether $K_X + \Delta$ is pseudo-effective, and identify the output.
3. (B) Let $(X, \Delta)$ be a smooth surface with a boundary $\Delta$ whose support is a cycle of rational curves. Show directly that every sequence of $(K_X + \Delta)$-negative divisorial contractions preserves the number of lc centres, or lowers it, and describe when it lowers it.
4. (B) Show that the toric $K$-program with scaling of Proposition 9.1.20 satisfies the conclusion of Proposition 9.3.10: list the intervals of $t$ on which $X^+$ and $\mathbb{P}(1, 1, 2, 3)$ are weak log canonical models of $K + tH'$.
5. (B) Show that in Theorem 9.3.11 the hypothesis that $\Delta$ is big cannot simply be dropped from the proof: find the step that fails when $\Delta = 0$ and $K_X$ is pseudo-effective but not big.
6. (C) Let $(X, \Delta)$ be a $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial klt surface pair and $C$ ample with $K_X + \Delta + C$ nef. Prove that every $(K_X + \Delta)$-program with scaling of $C$ terminates, without Theorem 9.1.8, and compare the number of steps with $\rho(X)$.
7. (C) Construct a $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial klt pair and a scaling divisor $C$ such that the program with scaling of $C$ has two different runs, differing at a tie, with non-isomorphic outputs, and show that both outputs are log terminal models or both are Mori fibre spaces, as Corollary 9.3.12 requires.
8. (C) In the outline of Theorem 9.3.13, step 4, show that a $(K_X + \Delta')$-negative curve meets $\lfloor \Delta' \rfloor$ when $K_X + \Delta' \sim_{\mathbb{R}, U} D'$ with $D' \ge 0$ supported in $\lfloor \Delta' \rfloor$, and deduce that a program in which special termination holds stops.
