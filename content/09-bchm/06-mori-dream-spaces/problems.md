## Problems

Computations of effective, movable and nef cones, Mori chambers, Cox rings and runs of the program on Mori dream spaces.

1. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}(1, 1, 2)$. Show that $\operatorname{Cl}(X) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ and $\operatorname{Pic}(X) \cong 2\mathbb{Z}$, compute the $\operatorname{Cl}$-graded ring $k[x_0, x_1, x_2]$ with degrees $1, 1, 2$, and show that $\operatorname{Cox}(X)$ in the sense of Definition 9.6.2 is the Veronese subring of even degrees, generated by the four elements $x_0^2$, $x_0 x_1$, $x_1^2$, $x_2$ of degree $2$.
2. (B) For $\mathbb{F}_2$ with $D = aC_- + bF$, write down $h^0(\mathbb{F}_2, D)$ for all $a, b \ge 0$ from the Cox ring $k[x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4]$ whose variables have the classes $F$, $C_-$, $F$ and $C_- + 2F$, and recover the two chambers of Example 9.6.6 for $n = 2$.
3. (B) On $S_2$ list, for each of the twelve classes $L$, $L - E_1$, $L - E_2$, $E_1$, $E_2$, $E_{12}$, $2L - E_1$, $2L - E_2$, $L + E_1$, $L + E_2$, $2L - E_1 - E_2$ and $E_1 + E_2$, the Zariski decomposition and the ample model, and place each class in the chambers of Example 9.6.7.
4. (B) Let $X = S_3$, the plane blown up at three non-collinear points. Show that the six $(-1)$-curves form a hexagon in which adjacent curves meet once, compute $\operatorname{Nef}(X)$ as the dual cone, and show that its extremal rays are $L$, $2L - E_1 - E_2 - E_3$ and the three classes $L - E_i$.
5. (B) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^3$ at a point, with exceptional divisor $E$ and $H$ the pullback of a plane. Show that $\overline{\mathrm{Eff}}(X) = \langle E, H - E \rangle$, $\operatorname{Nef}(X) = \langle H, H - E \rangle$, and determine the two Mori chambers and their models.
6. (B) For $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$, show that $\overline{\mathrm{Eff}}(X) = \operatorname{Nef}(X)$ and compute the ample models of all faces of this cone.
7. (C) Let $X = S_2$, $D = 4L - 2E_1 - 3E_2$ and $H = 4L - E_1 - 2E_2$. Show that $H$ is ample and $D$ is big but not nef, run the program with scaling of $H$, and show that it contracts $E_{12}$ at $t = 1$ and stops on $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ with $D$ ample there; locate $D$ in the chambers of Example 9.6.7.
8. (C) Let $X$ be a smooth projective surface with $H^1(X, \mathcal{O}_X) = 0$. Show that $X$ is a Mori dream space if and only if $\operatorname{Nef}(X)$ is spanned by finitely many semiample classes. Then let $X$ be a K3 surface with $\operatorname{NS}(X)$ of rank $2$ and intersection form $2x^2 - 6y^2$: show that $X$ has no curves of negative self-intersection and no nonzero classes of square zero, that $\operatorname{Nef}(X)$ is the closure of the positive cone, with irrational extremal rays, and conclude that $X$ is not a Mori dream space.
