## Problems

Each problem computes a restricted algebra, an asymptotic fixed divisor or an adjoint algebra on a toric or a surface example.

1. (B) Generalize Example 9.2.4 to the toric flip of $\mathbb{Z}^{p+q}$ with relation weights $(1, \dots, 1, -1, \dots, -1)$, with $p$ entries $1$ and $q$ entries $-1$, $p > q \ge 1$. Show that $X^- \to Z$ is a pl flipping contraction for $S = D_{u}$ with $u$ one of the vectors of weight $-1$, identify $S$ and $S^+$, and compute $R_S$.
2. (B) In Problem 1, compute $\operatorname{res}_S R(X^-, K_{X^-} + S)$ and compare it with $R(S / T, K_S)$, where $T$ is the image of $S$ in $Z$.
3. (B) On $X = \operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2$ with $S$ the strict transform of a line through $p$, let $\Delta = S + A + bE$ with $A$ as in Example 9.2.11 and $0 \le b < 1$. Find all $b$ for which $\Theta \ne \Omega$, and compute $\Theta$ for each.
4. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{F}_2$ with negative section $\sigma$, fibre $f$ and $S$ a section disjoint from $\sigma$, so $S \sim \sigma + 2f$. For $\Delta = S + A + b\sigma$ with $A$ a general ample $\mathbb{Q}$-divisor of small degree, compute $F(K_X + \Delta, S)$ and $\Theta$.
5. (B) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$, $S$ a fibre of the first projection and $D = aF_1 + bF_2$ with $a, b \ge 0$ integers. Compute $\operatorname{res}_S R(X, D)$ and show that it equals $R(S, D|_S)$; then find a divisor $D$ on $\operatorname{Bl}_p \mathbb{P}^2$ and a curve $S$ for which the restricted algebra is a proper subalgebra of $R(S, D|_S)$ in infinitely many degrees.
6. (C) Let $X$ be a smooth projective 3-fold and $\Delta = S + A + B$ as in Theorem 9.2.8 with $K_X + \Delta$ nef and big. Using Lemma 9.2.10 and the base point free theorem on $X$, show that $\operatorname{res}_S R(X, k(K_X + \Delta)) = R(S, k(K_S + \Omega))$ and that both are finitely generated.
7. (C) Compute the restricted algebra $\operatorname{res}_S R(X^-, 2(K_{X^-} + S))$ for the Francia flip with $S = D_1$, using the toric description of the sections of $\mathcal{O}_{X^-}(mD_1)$ by lattice points, and check that it agrees with $R(S / \mathbb{A}^2, 2K_S)$ in every degree.
8. (C) Show that the conclusion of Corollary 9.2.9 fails if $\Theta$ is replaced by $\Omega$ in Example 9.2.11, and find the smallest $m$ divisible by $4$ for which $|m(K_S + \Omega)|$ contains a divisor not in $|m(K_X + \Delta)|_S$.
