# Pl flips and the extension theorem

This chapter proves the first arrow of the induction, $F_{n-1} \Rightarrow A_n$: finite generation in dimension $n - 1$ gives the existence of pl flips in dimension $n$. The argument is due to Hacon and McKernan, and it replaces the 3-fold complements of Shokurov, used in Volume 8 for Theorem 8.3.9, by an extension theorem for pluricanonical sections proved with multiplier ideals.

Section 1 recalls pl flipping contractions and Shokurov's reduction, Theorem 8.3.5, which holds in every dimension, and defines the restricted algebra of a divisor. It proves that a pl flip exists exactly when the restricted algebra of $S$ is finitely generated in the form needed later, and works the standard toric 4-fold pl flip, where $S$ is the blow-up of $\mathbb{A}^3$ at the origin and every algebra can be written down.

Section 2 is the extension theorem. It defines restricted linear systems and their asymptotic fixed divisors, proves the subadditivity that makes the limit exist, states the theorem of Hacon and McKernan, and draws the consequence that matters: the restricted algebra of $K_X + \Delta$ is, up to a shift and a Veronese subalgebra, the adjoint algebra of a pair $(S, \Theta)$ of dimension one less. The case of a nef and big divisor is proved in full from the vanishing theorems of Volume 5, and the general proof is outlined with its key steps. A surface example shows a nonzero asymptotic fixed divisor and the boundary $\Theta$ that it produces.

Section 3 assembles the proof of $A_n$ from $F_{n-1}$, marked as an outline where it leans on the rationality of $\Theta$, which is a statement about the Shokurov polytope treated in Chapter 4. It recovers the 3-fold theorem of Volume 8 and computes the adjoint algebras of the Francia flip and of the 4-fold flip of Section 1.

The problems compute restricted linear systems, asymptotic fixed divisors and adjoint algebras on toric and surface examples.
