## Reading paths

A path is a list of chapters, not of volumes. No reader reads all ten thousand pages. Pick the path that matches what you know and what you want. The page counts are estimates from the plan of the handbook. Most of the chapters on a path are not written yet; the site shows each path with a checkbox per chapter.

| Path | For | Chapters | Pages | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full | a strong undergraduate | P0 to P8 in order, with P6 in parallel with P5 or P7; then Volumes 0 to 8 | about 7,000 | four to five semesters for P, two more for 0 to 8 |
| Fast | a student who knows Hartshorne II, III and V, or Vakil | P0 Ch. 1, 2; P1 Ch. 10, 11; P3 Ch. 8, 9; P4 Ch. 6, 8, 9; P5 Ch. 8, 11, 12; P6 Ch. 6, 8; P7 all; P8 Ch. 1 to 5 and 9 to 13; then Volumes 0 to 8 | 1,350 of P, then 2,940 of the program | two semesters for P |
| Surfaces first | a student who wants Volume 2 only | P0; P1 Ch. 1 to 9; P3; P4 Ch. 1 to 7; P5; P8 Ch. 4, 9 to 11; then Volumes 0 and 2 | about 2,400 | three semesters |
| Toric first | a student from combinatorics or convex geometry, or a Lean contributor to the toric MMP | P0 Ch. 1 to 4; P8 Ch. 6 to 8; Volume 3 with its Lean parts; then P3 Ch. 8 to 11 and Volume 4 Ch. 1 to 3 | about 900 | one semester |
| Complex-analytic | a student who knows Huybrechts | skip P6; P1 Ch. 4 to 11, P3 and P4 quickly; Griffiths-Harris for much of P5; then the fast path from P7 | about 1,800 | two semesters |
| Arithmetic | a student who knows Liu or Silverman | P5 Ch. 5 to 12; P7; P8; then Volumes 0 to 8, and Volume 11 early | about 1,300 of P | two semesters |
| BCHM seminar | a student who knows Kollár-Mori Ch. 1 to 3 | Volume 4 Ch. 1 to 4; Volume 5; Volume 7; Volume 8 Ch. 1 and 2; Volume 9 section by section | about 1,400 | one semester |
| Boundedness | a student after the BCHM path | Volume 10 Ch. 1 to 3; Volume 12; Volume 16 Ch. 1 | about 650 | one semester |
| Shape first | a reader who wants the program before the proofs | Volume 0; the first chapter of each of Volumes 1 to 12; Volume 17 | about 400 | four weeks |
| Lean contributor | a person who wants to write Lean for the project | Volume 0 Ch. 4; P3 Ch. 8 and 9 with their Lean parts; Volume 4 Ch. 1; the Lean parts of Volume 3 | about 300 | four weeks |

The BCHM seminar path follows a course of Chenyang Xu (Peking University, 2015). The boundedness path follows a course of Joaquín Moraga (UCLA, 2021 to 2022). The shape first path follows the lectures of Caucher Birkar at the Collège de France in 2011, written up as Bir12.

The first chapter of each program volume is written to be read alone. It states the main theorems of the volume, gives the examples, and says what the rest of the volume proves. Volume 1 Chapter 1 is an overview of the whole program, and it is the second stop on the shape first path.

### Exercises

1. (A) A student knows Atiyah-Macdonald and Hartshorne Chapter I, and wants to read the proof of Castelnuovo's contraction theorem. Write a path for this student from the table.
2. (A) List the P chapters that are on both the fast path and the surfaces first path.
