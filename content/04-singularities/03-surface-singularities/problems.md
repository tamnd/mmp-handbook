## Problems

Each problem resolves a surface singularity by hand, finds the dual graph of the minimal resolution, and computes the discrepancies by the linear system of Section 1. Where the germ is toric, check the answer against the formula $a(v) = \langle m_\sigma, v \rangle - 1$ of Volume 3, Chapter 3.

1. (A) For each $q = 1, \dots, 6$, find the continued fraction of $\frac{7}{q}$ and the discrepancies of $\frac17(1,q)$. Show that $\frac1n(1,q) \cong \frac1n(1,q')$ when $q q' \equiv 1 \pmod n$, and that the two chains are the same chain in reverse order. Deduce that the values of $\operatorname{discrep}$ for $q = 1, \dots, 6$ are $-\frac57, -\frac47, -\frac37, -\frac47, -\frac37, 0$.
2. (A) Let $X = \{xy = z^4\}$, the $A_3$ singularity. Show that the blow-up of the origin has as exceptional set two smooth rational curves that meet at one point, and that this point is an $A_1$ singularity of the blow-up, with local equation $x_1 y_1 = z^2$. Blow up that point, and show that the result is the minimal resolution, a chain of three $(-2)$-curves. Check the chain in the toric picture: $X$ is $\operatorname{Cone}(e_2, 4e_1 - 3e_2)$, the rays are $(1, 0)$, $(2, -1)$, $(3, -2)$, and $m_\sigma = (1, 1)$ gives $a = 0$ for each.
3. (B) Let $X = \{x^2 + y^3 + z^3 = 0\}$, the $D_4$ singularity. Show that the exceptional set of the blow-up of the origin is one smooth rational curve $E_0$, the reduced line $\{x = 0\}$ of the tangent cone. In the chart $x = x' y$, $z = z' y$, show that the strict transform is $\{x'^2 + y(1 + z'^3) = 0\}$, with three singular points of type $A_1$ on $E_0$, at $z'^3 = -1$. Deduce that the minimal resolution has the graph $D_4$, with $E_0$ in the centre, and that $X$ is canonical.
4. (B) Find the cyclic quotient $\frac1n(1,q)$ whose minimal resolution is a chain with self-intersections $-2, -3, -2$. Compute its discrepancies by the linear system and by the toric formula, and check that they agree. Compute its index and its index one cover by Exercise 3 of Section 3.3. Explain why the chain is symmetric in terms of the relation $q^2 \equiv 1 \pmod n$.
5. (C) Let $X = \{x^2 + y^3 + z^4 = 0\}$, the $E_6$ singularity. Show that the exceptional set of the blow-up of the origin is one smooth rational curve $E_0$, and that the blow-up has exactly one singular point $p$, on $E_0$, in the chart $x = x_1 z$, $y = y_1 z$. Show that near $p$ the blow-up is $\{x_1^2 + z^2 + y_1^3 z = 0\}$, and that the coordinates $s = x_1 + i w$, $t = x_1 - i w$ with $w = z + \frac12 y_1^3$ put it in the form $st = \frac14 y_1^6$, an $A_5$ singularity. Show that $E_0$ is the curve $s = \frac{i}{2} y_1^3$, $t = -\frac{i}{2} y_1^3$, and use the toric resolution of $A_5$ to show that its strict transform meets only the middle curve of the chain. Deduce the graph $E_6$, and that every discrepancy of $X$ is 0.
