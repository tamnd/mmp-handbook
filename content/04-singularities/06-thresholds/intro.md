# Thresholds and minimal log discrepancies

The classes of Chapter 2 sort pairs into a few boxes. This chapter attaches two numbers to a pair, which measure how far it is from the edge of a box. The *log canonical threshold* of a divisor $D$ with respect to a pair $(X, \Delta)$ is the largest $t$ for which $(X, \Delta + tD)$ is still lc. The *minimal log discrepancy* at a point $x$ is the smallest log discrepancy of a divisor with centre $x$. Both are computed on one log resolution, or on one toric model, and both enter the termination of flips through the conjectures of Shokurov.

Section 1 defines the log canonical threshold of a divisor and of an ideal, proves that a log resolution computes it as a minimum of finitely many ratios, and deduces that the local threshold is lower semicontinuous. The examples are the cusp $y^2 = x^3$, with threshold $\frac56$, and two arrangements of planes in $\mathbb{A}^3$: four general planes through the origin have threshold $\frac34$, while the four planes $xyz(x - y) = 0$ have threshold $\frac23$, computed along the line where three of them meet. Section 2 proves Howald's theorem: the threshold of a monomial ideal is $\frac1t$, where $(t, \dots, t)$ is the point where the diagonal leaves the Newton polyhedron. A general member of the ideal has threshold $\min\{1, \frac1t\}$, which gives the threshold $\min\{1, \frac1a + \frac1b\}$ of $x^a + y^b$.

Section 3 defines the minimal log discrepancy, computes it for snc pairs, where a smooth point has $\operatorname{mld} = \dim X$, and proves the toric formula: at the fixed point of an affine toric variety it is the least value of $m_\sigma$ on the interior lattice points of the cone. This gives $\operatorname{mld} = \min_k \frac{k + \overline{ka}}{r}$ for $\frac1r(1, a)$, $\operatorname{mld} = 1$ for $A_n$ and $\operatorname{mld} = 1 + \frac1r$ for the terminal quotients $\frac1r(1, -1, a)$. The cone over a curve of genus $g$ by a line bundle of degree $d$, with discrepancy $-1 - \frac{2g-2}{d}$ by Corollary 4.2.20, has $\operatorname{mld} = \frac{2 - 2g}{d}$ when $g \le 1$.

Section 4 states the ascending chain condition for thresholds, proved by Hacon, McKernan and Xu, and the two conjectures of Shokurov and Ambro on minimal log discrepancies, the ACC and the lower semicontinuity. It lists the cases that are known, proves the abstract lemma behind Shokurov's reduction of termination to these two conjectures, and states the reduction. The problems compute thresholds and minimal log discrepancies of explicit curves, ideals and quotients.
