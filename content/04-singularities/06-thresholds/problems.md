## Problems

Each problem computes a threshold or a minimal log discrepancy of an explicit pair, by a log resolution as in Section 1, by the Newton polyhedron as in Section 2, by the toric formula of Section 3, or tests a statement of Section 4. Give the divisor or the lattice point that computes each number.

1. (A) Let $D = \{x^2 y^3 = 0\} \subset \mathbb{A}^2$. Show that $\operatorname{lct}(\mathbb{A}^2; D) = \frac13$, and compute $\operatorname{lct}_x(\mathbb{A}^2; D)$ at every closed point $x$: it is $1$ off $D$, $\frac12$ on the line $x = 0$ away from the origin, and $\frac13$ on the line $y = 0$. Check that the function is lower semicontinuous, as Corollary 4.6.4 says.
2. (A) Show that the curve $y^2 = x^5$ has threshold $\frac{7}{10}$, once by Howald's theorem and once by an explicit log resolution, and find the exceptional divisor that computes it.
3. (A) Draw the Newton polyhedron of $\mathfrak{a} = (x^4, x^2 y, y^3)$. Show that the diagonal leaves it on the edge from $(2, 1)$ to $(0, 3)$, at $t = \frac32$, and that $\operatorname{lct}(\mathfrak{a}) = \frac23$. Find the weight vector $v$ with $\operatorname{lct}(\mathfrak{a}) = \frac{\langle 1, v\rangle}{\varphi(v)}$.
4. (A) Show that $\operatorname{mld}$ of $\frac17(1, 3)$ is $\frac47$: list the six values $\frac{k + \overline{3k}}{7}$ for $k = 1, \dots, 6$, which are $\frac47, \frac87, \frac57, \frac97, \frac67, \frac{10}7$, and find the toric divisor that computes the minimum.
5. (A) Show that $\operatorname{mld}$ of $\frac15(1, 1, 2)$ at the fixed point is $\frac45$, and compare with Problem 1 of Chapter 4, where the same point appears as klt and not canonical.
6. (B) Let $D = \{xy(x - y)(x - 2y) z = 0\} \subset \mathbb{A}^3$. Show that the four planes through the $z$-axis $L$ give $\operatorname{lct}(\mathbb{A}^3; D) = \frac12$, computed by the blow-up of $L$, and that the blow-up of the origin gives only $\frac35$. Check that the blow-up of $L$ is a log resolution.
7. (B) Let $C = \{y^2 = x^3\}$ be the cusp. Use the log resolution of Example 4.6.7 to show that, for $0 \le t \le \frac56$,
$$\operatorname{mld}(0; \mathbb{A}^2, tC) = \begin{cases} 2 - 2t & t \le \frac34, \\ 5 - 6t & t \ge \frac34, \end{cases}$$
and that it is $-\infty$ for $t > \frac56$. Which exceptional divisor computes it on each interval?
8. (B) For the divisor $D = \{xyz(x - y) = 0\}$ of Example 4.6.9, compute $\operatorname{lct}_x(\mathbb{A}^3; D)$ at every closed point $x$. Show that it takes only the values $1$ and $\frac23$, and that the set where it is $\frac23$ is the line $x = y = 0$.
9. (B) Show that the monomial ideal $(x^a, y^b)$ has threshold $\frac1a + \frac1b$, with no minimum with $1$, and that the set of these numbers for $a, b \ge 1$ satisfies the ACC. Find its accumulation points, and compare with Example 4.6.34.
10. (B) Show that the cone over a rational normal curve of degree $d$ is the quotient $\frac1d(1, 1)$, and check that Example 4.6.21 and Example 4.6.25 give the same minimal log discrepancy $\frac2d$.
11. (B) Let $S \subset \mathbb{A}^3$ be a smooth surface, $W \subset S$ a smooth curve and $0 \le c \le 1$. Compute $\operatorname{mld}(W; \mathbb{A}^3, cS)$ and $\operatorname{mld}(S; \mathbb{A}^3, cS)$, and show that $\operatorname{mld}(Z) + \dim Z$ is the same for $Z = S$, $Z = W$ and a closed point $Z \in W$. Explain, as in Example 4.6.36, why the plain function $Z \mapsto \operatorname{mld}(Z)$ is not lower semicontinuous on all scheme points.
12. (C) Show that Lemma 4.6.32 fails without lower semicontinuity. Let $L = P = [0, 1]$, let $a(\frac1k) = 1 + \frac1k$ for $k \ge 2$ and $a(p) = 2$ at all other points, and let $S = \{1 + \frac1k : k \ge 2\} \cup \{2\}$. Show that $S$ satisfies the ACC, that $a$ is not lower semicontinuous at $0$, and that one state with a step to itself satisfies the other hypotheses of the lemma and has an infinite sequence of steps.
