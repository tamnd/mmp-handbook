## Problems

These problems use only the convex geometry of this chapter. Most of them are hand computations, and the later chapters use their answers.

1. (A) Find the dual cone, the faces and the Hilbert basis of $\sigma = \operatorname{Cone}(e_1, e_1 + 3 e_2) \subset \mathbb{R}^2$.
2. (A) Show that $\operatorname{Cone}(e_2, n e_1 - q e_2)$ and $\operatorname{Cone}(e_2, n e_1 - q' e_2)$ are carried to each other by an element of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ when $q q' \equiv 1 \pmod n$.
3. (B) Let $\Sigma$ be the fan of $\mathbb{P}^2$ of Example 3.1.24, with rays $e_1$, $e_2$, $-e_1 - e_2$. Let $\Sigma'$ be the star subdivision of $\Sigma$ at $e_1 + e_2$. List the cones of $\Sigma'$. Then find an element of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ that carries $\Sigma'$ to the fan of $\mathbb{F}_1$ of Example 3.1.25.
4. (B) Let $\sigma = \operatorname{Cone}(e_1, e_2, e_1 + e_3, e_2 + e_3) \subset \mathbb{R}^3$. Show that $\sigma$ is a cone over a square, and find an element of $\mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb{Z})$ that carries it to the cone of Example 3.1.7.
5. (C) Let $\sigma = \operatorname{Cone}(e_2, n e_1 - q e_2)$ with $0 < q < n$ and $\gcd(n, q) = 1$. Show that the Hilbert basis of $\sigma^\vee \cap M$ is given by the continued fraction of $n/(n - q)$, in the same way as Exercise 3 of Section 4 gives the Hilbert basis of $\sigma \cap N$ from $n/q$.
6. (C) Find a complete fan in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with a cone that is not simplicial, and give a simplicial refinement of it with no new rays.
