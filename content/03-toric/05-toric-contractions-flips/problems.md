## Problems

Each problem asks for the contraction or the flip of an extremal ray, computed from wall relations and circuits.

1. (A) On the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at its three fixed points, with the six rays of Problem 1 of Chapter 4, find the extremal rays of $NE(X)$, show that each is divisorial with $J_-$ a single ray, and identify the six contractions.
2. (B) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^3$ along a torus-invariant line, the star subdivision at $e_1 + e_2$ of the fan of $\mathbb{P}^3$. Find the relations of the walls, the two extremal rays, and their contractions; show that one contraction is the blow-down to $\mathbb{P}^3$ and the other a fibration onto $\mathbb{P}^1$ with fibres $\mathbb{P}^2$ and $J_- = \emptyset$.
3. (B) Let $q = (2, 1, -1, -1)$ and $N = \mathbb{Z}^4 / \mathbb{Z} q$. Find the multiplicities of the cones of $T_+$ and $T_-$, the intersection numbers of the two flipping curves with the $D_i$ and with $K$, and decide which side is the $K$-flip of the other.
4. (B) In the $(1, 2, 3)$ weighted blow-up $X$ of Example 3.5.18, compute the domain fan of a supporting divisor of the ray of the curves in $E$ and check that it is the fan of $\mathbb{P}^3$. Then compute the fan $\Sigma^+$ of the flip of $C_{23}$ and its walls, and show that $R^+$ is one of the two extremal rays of $NE(X^+)$; find the other.
5. (C) Let $\Sigma$ be complete simplicial and projective, and $R$ an extremal ray with $|J_-| \ge 2$. Show that every maximal cone of $\Sigma$ that contains $\sigma_-$ lies in a circuit cone of $\Sigma_R$, that each circuit cone contains exactly $|J_+|$ of them, and that the circuit cones correspond to the torus-fixed points of $V(\operatorname{Cone}(Z)) \subset Z_R$. Count them in Example 3.5.25.
