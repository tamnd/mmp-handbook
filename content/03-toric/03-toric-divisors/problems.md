## Problems

Each problem is a computation by hand with the divisors, the canonical divisor and the discrepancies of this chapter.

1. (A) Let $\sigma = \operatorname{Cone}(e_2, 5 e_1 - 2 e_2)$, the cone of $\frac15(1,2)$. Show that $m_\sigma = (\frac35, 1)$ and that the index of $K_{U_\sigma}$ is 5. The minimal resolution adds the rays through $(1, 0)$ and $(3, -1)$ (Example 3.1.32). Show that the exceptional curves $E_1$, $E_2$ have $E_1^2 = -3$ and $E_2^2 = -2$, and that their discrepancies are $-\frac25$ and $-\frac15$. Check the answer with the adjunction formula $K \cdot E_i = -2 - E_i^2$.
2. (B) On the Hirzebruch surface $\mathbb{F}_n$, use $-K \sim 2 D_2 + (n + 2) f$ and the fact that the cone of curves of $\mathbb{F}_n$ is spanned by $D_2$ and $f$ (Cox-Little-Schenck, Chapter 6) to show that $-K_{\mathbb{F}_n}$ is ample if and only if $n \le 1$ and nef if and only if $n \le 2$.
3. (B) Let $\Sigma$ be the fan of $\mathbb{P}(1,1,1,2)$ of Example 3.3.25, with rays $e_1$, $e_2$, $e_3$ and $u_0 = -e_1 - e_2 - 2 e_3$. Show that $K \sim -5 D_0$ in $\operatorname{Cl}(X_\Sigma) \cong \mathbb{Z}$. On the singular cone $\operatorname{Cone}(e_1, e_2, u_0)$, show that $m_\sigma = (1, 1, -\frac32)$, so that $K$ has index 2. Show that every lattice point $v$ of this cone other than $0$ and the three generators has $\langle m_\sigma, v \rangle \ge \frac32$, with equality at $v = -e_3 = \frac12 (e_1 + e_2 + u_0)$. Deduce that the discrepancy of $-e_3$ is $\frac12$ and that $X_\Sigma$ is terminal.
4. (B) Let $\sigma \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ be the cone over the hexagon with vertices $(1,0)$, $(1,1)$, $(0,1)$, $(-1,0)$, $(-1,-1)$, $(0,-1)$ at height 1. Show that $U_\sigma$ is Gorenstein and canonical but not terminal. Show that the star subdivision at $(0, 0, 1)$ is a crepant resolution, and that its exceptional divisor is the toric surface of the fan in $\mathbb{R}^2$ with the six vertices as rays, the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at the three fixed points.
5. (C) Let $\Sigma_+$ be the small resolution of the cone over a square with maximal cones $\operatorname{Cone}(u_1, u_2, u_3)$ and $\operatorname{Cone}(u_1, u_3, u_4)$, and $C = V(\operatorname{Cone}(u_1, u_3)) \cong \mathbb{P}^1$. Use the relation $u_2 + u_4 = u_1 + u_3$ and the wall formula for intersection numbers (Cox-Little-Schenck, Chapter 6) to show that $D_2 \cdot C = D_4 \cdot C = 1$ and $D_1 \cdot C = D_3 \cdot C = -1$. Deduce that $K \cdot C = 0$ and that the normal bundle of $C$ is $\mathcal{O}(-1) \oplus \mathcal{O}(-1)$. Do the same for $\Sigma_-$, and show that $D_1$ is negative on the curve of $\Sigma_+$ and positive on the curve of $\Sigma_-$.
