## Problems

Each problem runs a toric minimal model program, or one of its ingredients, from the wall relations of the fan.

1. (A) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^3$ at the fixed point $\gamma_{\operatorname{Cone}(e_1, e_2, e_3)}$, the star subdivision at $e_1 + e_2 + e_3$. Find $NE(X)$, show that it has one divisorial and one fibre type ray, and list the toric $K$-MMPs of $X$.
2. (A) Compute the mld at the fixed point of $\frac17(1, 3)$ with Theorem 3.6.10, and find all primitive interior lattice points of the cone at which the minimum is attained.
3. (B) Let $q = (1, 1, 1, -1, -1)$ and $X_\pm$ the two sides of the circuit, as in Exercise 3 of Section 3 of Chapter 5. Use Proposition 3.6.1 to compute $a(E_v, X_-) - a(E_v, X_+)$ at the lattice point $v = w_1 + w_2 + w_3 = w_4 + w_5$, and check that the discrepancy goes up under the $K$-flip.
4. (B) Show that the weighted blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^3$ with weights $(1, 1, 3)$ has a $K$-trivial small ray, compute its flop $X^+$ from the circuit of the wall, and list the singular fixed points of $X$ and of $X^+$ with their multiplicities.
5. (B) Let $D = a D_0 + b E$ on the $(1, 2, 3)$ blow-up of Section 3. Divide the $(a, b)$-plane into the regions where the toric $D$-MMP starts with the flip of $C_{23}$, with the contraction of $E$, or stops at once, and describe the end of the program in each region.
6. (C) Let $\sigma$ be a full-dimensional simplicial cone and $\Delta = \sum_\rho d_\rho D_\rho$ with $0 \le d_\rho < 1$. Show that $\operatorname{mld}(\gamma_\sigma; U_\sigma, \Delta) \le n - \sum_\rho d_\rho$, with equality if and only if $\sigma$ is smooth, using the point $\sum_\rho u_\rho$ for the bound and a nonzero lattice point of the box $\{\sum \lambda_\rho u_\rho : 0 \le \lambda_\rho < 1\}$, plus the $u_\rho$ with $\lambda_\rho = 0$, for the converse.
