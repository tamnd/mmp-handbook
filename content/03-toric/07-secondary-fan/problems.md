## Problems

Each problem works in the space of classes $G_U$ of a fixed list of vectors.

1. (A) Compute the Gale dual and the chambers for the rays of the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at two torus-fixed points, and match each chamber with a toric surface.
2. (A) For the rays of $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$, show that there is exactly one chamber and find the faces of $\operatorname{Cone}(B)$ that the three projections give.
3. (B) Let $U$ be the rays of the weighted blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^3$ with weights $(1, 1, 3)$. Find the chambers, identify the flop of Problem 4 of Chapter 6 as a wall between two chambers that use all the vectors, and locate $-K$.
4. (B) Show that for $n = 2$ every chamber of a list $U$ that uses all the vectors is the nef cone of the unique complete fan with rays $U$, so the movable cone of a toric surface is its nef cone.
5. (B) Let $U$ be the rays of $\mathbb{P}^n$ together with $v = e_1 + \dots + e_n$, $n \ge 2$. Show that there are exactly two chambers, those of $\mathbb{P}^n$ and of its blow-up at a fixed point, and that the outer facet of the second is the facet of the fibre-type ray of the projection of the blow-up to $\mathbb{P}^{n-1}$.
6. (C) Let $\Sigma$ be a projective $U$-fan that uses all the vectors and $D$ a big divisor. Show that every toric $D$-MMP from $X_\Sigma$ ends with a minimal model in a chamber that contains $d$, and that this chamber is unique when $d$ lies outside the walls of the secondary fan.
