## Problems

Each problem is a computation by hand with the charts, orbits and morphisms of this chapter.

1. (A) Let $\Sigma$ be the fan of $\mathbb{P}(1,1,n)$ of Example 3.1.26. Write down the three charts of $X_\Sigma$, and show that two of them are $\mathbb{A}^2$ and the third is $U_{\sigma_n}$.
2. (A) Show that $\mathbb{P}(1,1,n)$ is the projective cone over the rational normal curve of degree $n$: embed it in $\mathbb{P}^{n+1}$ with the characters of the lattice points of the triangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(1,0)$ and $(1,n)$, after a change of basis.
3. (B) Let $\sigma = \operatorname{Cone}(e_2, 5 e_1 - 2 e_2)$, the cone of $\frac15(1,2)$. Find the Hilbert basis of $S_\sigma$, the embedding dimension of $U_\sigma$ at the vertex, and the minimal resolution of $U_\sigma$ with its two exceptional curves.
4. (B) Let $\Sigma$ be the fan in $\mathbb{R}^3$ whose maximal cones are the four cones $\operatorname{Cone}(u_i, u_{i+1}, w)$ of the star subdivision of the cone over a square at $w = (1, 1, 2)$. Show that $X_\Sigma \to U_\sigma$ is the blow-up of the vertex of $xz = yw$, that the exceptional divisor is $V(w) \cong \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$, and that it factors through both small resolutions $X_{\Sigma_+}$ and $X_{\Sigma_-}$.
5. (C) Let $\Sigma$ be a complete fan in $\mathbb{R}^2$ with rays $u_1, \dots, u_r$ in cyclic order. Show that $X_\Sigma$ is obtained from $\mathbb{P}^2$, $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ or some $\mathbb{F}_n$ by a sequence of toric blow-ups of fixed points when $\Sigma$ is smooth, and find such a sequence when the rays are $e_1$, $e_1 + e_2$, $e_2$, $-e_1$, $-e_1 - e_2$, $-e_2$.
