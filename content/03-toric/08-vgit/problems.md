## Problems

Each problem uses an action of $\mathbb{G}_m$ on $\mathbb{A}^r$ with weights $q$, under the assumptions of Section 2 unless it says otherwise.

1. (A) For $q = (1, 2, 3, -1)$, find the charts of $X_+$ and their multiplicities, the singular points of $X_+$ with their types, and the discrepancy of the exceptional divisor of $\pi_+$.
2. (A) Show that the weights $q = (a, b, -c, -d)$ with $a, b, c, d > 0$, $\gcd$ conditions as in Section 2 and $a + b = c + d$ all give flops, and find those for which both $X_+$ and $X_-$ are smooth.
3. (B) Let $q = (1, 1, -1, -1, -1)$. Show that $X_0$ is the affine cone over the Segre embedding of $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^2$, that $\pi_+$ contracts a $\mathbb{P}^1$ and $\pi_-$ a $\mathbb{P}^2$, and that $X_- \dashrightarrow X_+$ is a $K$-flip between smooth fourfolds. Which side is $K$-negative?
4. (B) Let $q$ have $J_- = \{k\}$ and $|J_+| \ge 2$. Show that $X_+$ is smooth if and only if $q_j = 1$ for all $j \in J_+$, and that then $X_0$ is the quotient $\frac{1}{c}(1, \dots, 1)$ of Example 3.3.40 with $c = |q_k|$, $X_+$ is the total space of $\mathcal{O}(-c)$ on $\mathbb{P}^{n-1}$, and $\pi_+$ is the blow-up of the vertex when $c = 1$.
5. (B) Show that the semistable locus $\mathbb{A}^r_+$ is the complement of the coordinate subspace $V(x_j : j \in J_+)$, and that $\phi_+ \colon \mathbb{A}^r_+ \to X_+$ is a geometric quotient: its fibres are single orbits, all of them closed in $\mathbb{A}^r_+$. Show by an example that the orbits are not closed in $\mathbb{A}^r$.
6. (C) Drop the assumption $J_0 = \emptyset$. Show that the coordinates $x_i$ with $q_i = 0$ split off, so that $X_\pm = X'_\pm \times \mathbb{A}^{|J_0|}$ for the action on the other coordinates, and translate this into the circuit data of Definition 3.5.10 with $J_0 \neq \emptyset$.
