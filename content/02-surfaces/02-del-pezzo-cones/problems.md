## Problems

Each problem is a computation by hand in the lattice $I^{1,r}$, with classes written as $a\ell - \sum_i b_i E_i$, together with the cone theorem and the counts of this chapter.

1. (A) List the $6$ runs of the program from $S_2$. Show that the contractions of $E_1$ and of $E_2$ lead to $F_1$, which has two runs each, and that the contraction of $\ell - E_1 - E_2$ leads to $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$, which has two runs, one for each projection. Check the count $m_2 = 6$ of Theorem 2.2.14.
2. (A) On $S_4$, show that $\operatorname{Nef}(S_4)$ has exactly $10$ extremal rays: the five conic classes $\ell - E_i$ and $2\ell - E_1 - E_2 - E_3 - E_4$, and the five classes $\ell$ and $2\ell - E_j - E_k - E_l$ with $\{j, k, l\} \subseteq \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$. For $x = 2\ell - E_2 - E_3 - E_4$, find four linearly independent $(-1)$-curves orthogonal to $x$.
3. (B) On $S_5$, show that the classes $x$ with $x^2 = 1$ and $-K \cdot x = 3$ that are nef are $\ell$, the ten classes $2\ell - E_i - E_j - E_k$, and the five classes $3\ell - 2E_i - \sum_{j \neq i} E_j$. Show that each is the pullback of the class of a line under a birational morphism $S_5 \to \mathbb{P}^2$, and that together with the $10$ conic classes they are the $26$ extremal rays of $\operatorname{Nef}(S_5)$.
4. (B) Show that the number of birational morphisms $S_r \to \mathbb{P}^2$, up to automorphisms of $\mathbb{P}^2$, is $|W_r| / r!$, and compute it for $3 \leq r \leq 8$: $2, 5, 16, 72, 576, 17280$. Explain why each such morphism is the end of exactly $r!$ runs, and recover the number $|W_r|$ of runs ending at $\mathbb{P}^2 \to \operatorname{pt}$ in Theorem 2.2.14.
5. (C) On the cubic surface $S_6$, show that $\operatorname{Nef}(S_6)$ has $99$ extremal rays: the $27$ conic classes of Example 2.2.17 and the $72$ classes of Problem 4. Show that a nef class $x$ with $x^2 = 1$ is orthogonal to six pairwise disjoint $(-1)$-curves, and that the conic class $\ell - E_1$ is orthogonal to the ten $(-1)$-curves in its reducible fibres.
6. (A) Let $\gamma$ be the Geiser involution of Example 2.2.11. Show that $\gamma(\ell) = 8\ell - 3\sum_{i=1}^{7} E_i$, that this class has square $1$ and $-K$-degree $3$, and that it is the class of the plane octics with triple points at $p_1, \dots, p_7$. Compute $\gamma(\ell - E_1 - E_2)$.
7. (A) Let $\beta$ be the Bertini involution of Example 2.2.11. Show that $\beta(\ell) = 17\ell - 6\sum_{i=1}^{8} E_i$ and $\beta(E_1) = 6\ell - 3E_1 - 2\sum_{j \neq 1} E_j$, and check that the second class is exceptional.
8. (B) On $S_7$, sort the $126$ conic classes by degree $a$ and show that the types are $(1; 1)$, $(2; 1^4)$, $(3; 2, 1^5)$, $(4; 2^3, 1^4)$ and $(5; 2^6, 1)$, with $7$, $35$, $42$, $35$ and $7$ classes. Show that the Geiser involution maps a conic class $F$ to $-F - 2K$ and exchanges the types of degree $a$ and $6 - a$.
9. (B) In Theorem 2.2.20, with the notation $D_v = E_9 + v + \frac12 |v|^2 F$ of Example 2.2.23, take $v = E_1 - E_2$. Show that $D_v = 3\ell - 2E_2 - E_3 - E_4 - E_5 - E_6 - E_7 - E_8$, the strict transform of the unique cubic through $p_3, \dots, p_8$ with a node at $p_2$, and check $D_v \cdot F = 1$ and $D_v \cdot E_9 = 0$.
10. (C) In the setting of Example 2.2.23, show that $D_v \cdot D_w = -1 + \frac12 |v - w|^2$ for $v, w \in E_8$. Check that this agrees with $D_v \cdot D_w \geq 0$ for $v \neq w$, since the minimal norm of $E_8$ is $2$, and deduce that the $(-1)$-curves meeting $E_9$ in one point correspond to the $2160$ vectors of norm $4$ in $E_8$.
