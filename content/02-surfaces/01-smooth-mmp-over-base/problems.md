## Problems

These problems compute relative cones, elementary transformations and relative minimal models by hand, using only the intersection numbers of the surfaces involved.

1. (A) Let $x \in F_n$ be a closed point over $c \in \mathbb{P}^1$ and $S = \operatorname{Bl}_x F_n$, with the morphism $S \to \mathbb{P}^1$. Find the two $K$-negative extremal rays of $NE(S/\mathbb{P}^1)$, and identify the two relative minimal models, separately for $x \in C_-$ and $x \notin C_-$.
2. (A) Let $T \subseteq \mathbb{P}^3$ be the quadric cone and $h \colon F_2 \to T$ the resolution of Example 2.1.8, with exceptional curve $E$. Let $S = \operatorname{Bl}_x F_2$ for a point $x$, with $f \colon S \to T$. Show that the run of $f$ over $T$ has exactly one step and ends at $F_2 \to T$, both for $x \in E$ and for $x \notin E$, and compute the intersection matrix of the two $f$-contracted curves when $x \in E$.
3. (A) Let $S_0 \subseteq \mathbb{P}^3$ be a smooth quintic, $\pi \colon S \to S_0$ the blow-up of a point with exceptional curve $E$, as in Example 2.1.20. Show that the base locus of $|K_S|$ is $E$, and that the rational map given by $|K_S|$ is $\pi$ followed by the inclusion $S_0 \subseteq \mathbb{P}^3$.
4. (B) Let $S$ be as in Problem 3, $y \in E$, and $S' = \operatorname{Bl}_y S$, with exceptional curve $E_y$ and strict transform $\tilde E$. Show that $K_{S'} = \pi'^* H + \tilde E + 2 E_y$, where $\pi' \colon S' \to S_0$ is the composite, that $E_y$ is the only $(-1)$-curve of $S'$, and that the absolute run on $S'$ contracts $E_y$ and then the image of $\tilde E$. Find the Zariski decomposition of $K_{S'}$.
5. (B) Let $p \colon S \to B$ be a fibration with a fibre $S_b = C_1 + \dots + C_n$, $n \geq 3$, a cycle of smooth rational curves with $C_i^2 = -2$ and $C_i \cdot C_{i+1} = 1$, indices modulo $n$. Verify part 2 of Lemma 2.1.3 directly: show that the intersection matrix is negative semidefinite, with kernel spanned by $(1, \dots, 1)$.
6. (B) Write a birational map $F_0 \dashrightarrow F_2$ over $\mathbb{P}^1$ as a composite of two elementary transformations, find a common resolution $W$ with $\rho(W) = 4$, and list the curves of $W$ contracted by each of the two morphisms $W \to F_0$ and $W \to F_2$, with their self-intersections.
7. (B) Let $X_9 \to \mathbb{P}^1$ be the rational elliptic surface, in characteristic different from $3$, of the Hesse pencil $\lambda (x^3 + y^3 + z^3) + \mu xyz = 0$, which has four singular fibres, each a triangle of lines. Show that $\rho(X_9/\mathbb{P}^1) = 9$, that $NE(X_9/\mathbb{P}^1)$ has exactly $12$ extremal rays, each spanned by a $(-2)$-curve, and that $K_{X_9}$ is $p$-nef. Show that the run of $X_9$ over $\mathbb{P}^1$ has no step, while every run over a point ends at a Mori fibre space.
8. (B) Let $S = \operatorname{Bl}_{P_1, \dots, P_4} \mathbb{P}^2$ with $P_1, \dots, P_4$ in general position, and $p \colon S \to \mathbb{P}^1$ the pencil of conics through the four points, as in Example P5.9.21, with $L_{ij}$ the strict transform of the line through $P_i$ and $P_j$. Show that $p$ has the three singular fibres $L_{12} + L_{34}$, $L_{13} + L_{24}$ and $L_{14} + L_{23}$, list the eight relative minimal models over $\mathbb{P}^1$, and show, using the squares of the images of the sections $E_1, \dots, E_4$, that four of them are $F_0$ and four are $F_1$.
9. (C) Let $p \colon S \to \mathbb{P}^1$ be a fibration whose fibres are $\mathbb{P}^1$ except for $s \geq 1$ fibres, each the sum of two $(-1)$-curves meeting once. Show that $p$ has exactly $2^s$ relative minimal models over $\mathbb{P}^1$, as surfaces with a map from $S$, that each is a Hirzebruch surface, and that exactly $2^{s-1}$ of them have $n$ even.
10. (C) Let $p \colon S \to B$ be a fibration over a curve of genus $g$ with $K_S \cdot F < 0$. Show that $K_S^2 + \rho(S) = 10 - 8g$, and that the number of reducible fibres, counted with the number of components minus one, is $\rho(S) - 2$. Check both statements for Example 2.1.21, Problem 8 and Problem 9.
