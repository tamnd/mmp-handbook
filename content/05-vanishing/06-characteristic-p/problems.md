## Problems

Each problem tests a vanishing statement in characteristic $p$, decides an F-splitting, or computes a test ideal or an F-pure threshold. Say which characteristic the answer depends on, and compare with characteristic $0$.

1. (A) Let $C$ be a smooth projective curve over a field of characteristic $p$ and $L$ an ample line bundle on $C$. Show that $H^1(C, K_C \otimes L) = 0$, and explain why Proposition 5.6.1 does not produce a counterexample on a curve.
2. (A) Decide for which $p$ the plane curve $xy(x + y) = 0$ is F-split at the origin, and compute its F-pure threshold for $p \equiv 1 \pmod 3$.
3. (A) Use Theorem 5.6.26 to compute $\tau((x^a, y^b)^c)$ for all $c > 0$, and list its jumping numbers. Compare with Corollary 5.4.30.
4. (B) Let $f = x^a + y^b$ with $a, b \geq 2$ coprime and $p \equiv 1 \pmod{ab}$. Show that $\operatorname{fpt}_0(f) = \frac1a + \frac1b$ when $\frac1a + \frac1b \leq 1$, by the method of Example 5.6.29.
5. (B) Let $f = x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + w^2$. Show that $f$ is F-split at the origin for every odd $p$, and compute $\operatorname{fpt}_0(f)$ for odd $p$.
6. (B) Let $f \in k[x, y, z, w]$ be a quartic form defining a smooth K3 surface $X \subset \mathbb{P}^3$. Show that the cone over $X$ is F-split at the vertex if and only if the coefficient of $(xyzw)^{p-1}$ in $f^{p-1}$ is nonzero modulo $p$, and show that for the Fermat quartic $x^4 + y^4 + z^4 + w^4$ this holds if and only if $p \equiv 1 \pmod 4$.
7. (B) Show that $\tau(\mathfrak{a}^c) \supseteq \mathfrak{a}^{\lceil c \rceil}$ for every nonzero ideal $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq S$ and $c \geq 0$, the analogue of part 1 of Proposition 5.5.4.
8. (B) Let $f \in \mathfrak{m}$ be nonzero. Show that $f$ is F-split at the origin if and only if $\nu_f(p) = p - 1$. Show that in this case $f^{q-1} \notin \mathfrak{m}^{[q]}$ for every $q = p^e$, by composing a splitting with itself, and deduce that $\operatorname{fpt}_0(f) = 1$. Hint: Theorem 5.6.11 and Proposition 5.6.25.
9. (B) Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety over a field of characteristic $p$ that lifts to $W_2(k)$ and has dimension $d < p$, and let $L$ be ample. Use Theorem P7.11.10 to show that $F^* \colon H^i(X, L^{-1}) \to H^i(X, L^{-p})$ is injective for $i < d$, and explain why Proposition 5.6.1 then gives no counterexample on $X$.
10. (C) Let $f = x^3 + y^3 + z^3$ and $p \equiv 2 \pmod 3$. Show that $\nu_f(p) = p - 2$, and compute $\operatorname{fpt}_0(f)$, comparing with $\operatorname{lct}_0(f) = 1$.
11. (C) Show that the test ideals of a monomial ideal satisfy the subadditivity $\tau((\mathfrak{a} \mathfrak{b})^c) \subseteq \tau(\mathfrak{a}^c) \, \tau(\mathfrak{b}^c)$ when $\mathfrak{a}$ and $\mathfrak{b}$ are monomial ideals in disjoint sets of variables, and compare with Lemma 5.4.6 and Theorem 5.4.7.
