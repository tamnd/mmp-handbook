## Problems

Each problem tests the injectivity, torsion freeness or vanishing theorems of the chapter on an explicit variety, fibration or pair.

1. (A) Let $f \colon X \to C$ be a minimal elliptic surface over a smooth projective curve, with $X$ smooth. Use Theorem 5.2.8 and Theorem 5.2.9 to show that $R^1 f_* \omega_X$ is a line bundle and $R^2 f_* \omega_X = 0$, and use Theorem 5.2.10 to show $H^1(C, R^1 f_* \omega_X \otimes \mathcal{O}_C(A)) = 0$ for every ample divisor $A$.
2. (A) Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety and $L$ a semiample line bundle with $\kappa(L) = 0$. Show that $L^m \cong \mathcal{O}_X$ for some $m \geq 1$, and that Corollary 5.2.4 for $L$ reduces to the statement that multiplication by a nonzero constant is injective.
3. (A) Let $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ and $L = \mathcal{O}(1, 0)$. Compute $h^1(X, \omega_X \otimes L^l)$ for every $l \geq 1$ and check that multiplication by a section of $L$ is injective on $H^1$, as Corollary 5.2.4 predicts.
4. (B) Let $X$ be the blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^2$ at a point, $E$ the exceptional curve and $f \colon X \to \mathbb{P}^1$ the projection from the point, with fibre $F$, and let $\Delta = c(F_1 + F_2)$ for two fibres and $0 \leq c < 1$. Find all divisors $N = aE + bF$ for which Theorem 5.2.10 applies, and compare with the range $b \geq -2$ found for $\Delta = 0$ in Exercise 6 of Section 2. Show that the boundary only shrinks the predicted range, while $H^1(\mathbb{P}^1, R^1 f_* \mathcal{O}_X(N))$ does not depend on $c$.
5. (B) Show that Theorem 5.2.3 implies Theorem 5.2.2 by taking $X$ smooth, and that Theorem 5.2.2 with $B$ a general member of $|mM|$ for a free $mM$ needs no snc hypothesis on $\operatorname{Supp}(\Delta + B)$ beyond that on $\Delta$, by Lemma P7.8.8.
6. (B) Let $f \colon X \to Z$ be a surjective morphism of normal projective varieties with $X$ klt, $\dim X = \dim Z$, and $f$ generically finite of degree $e$. Show that $f_* \mathcal{O}_X(K_X)$ is a torsion free sheaf of rank $e$ and that $R^j f_* \mathcal{O}_X(K_X) = 0$ for $j > 0$. Deduce the Grauert-Riemenschneider vanishing for a resolution of a klt variety from Corollary 5.2.11.
7. (B) Let $(X, \Delta)$ be a klt pair, $X$ projective, and $f \colon X \to Z$ a fibration onto a curve, with $-(K_X + \Delta) \sim_\mathbb{Q} f^* A$. Show that $R^j f_* \mathcal{O}_X$ is torsion free for every $j$, hence locally free, and that it vanishes for $j > \dim X - 1$. For $X = \mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ and $\Delta = \frac12 (F_1 + F_2 + F_3 + F_4)$, four fibres of the first projection, check that $-(K_X + \Delta)$ is a pullback from the second factor.
8. (C) Let $f \colon X \to Z$ be as in Theorem 5.2.10 with $A$ ample, and assume $\dim Z = 1$. Using Corollary 5.2.16, part 1, show that $h^i(X, \mathcal{O}_X(N)) = h^0(Z, R^i f_* \mathcal{O}_X(N))$ and that $\chi(X, \mathcal{O}_X(N)) = \sum_j (-1)^j h^0(Z, R^j f_* \mathcal{O}_X(N))$. Compute both sides for $X = E \times \mathbb{P}^1$, $f = p_2$ and $N = K_X + f^* \mathcal{O}(a)$.
9. (C) Let $\mu \colon X \to \mathbb{P}^2$ be the blow-up of a point and $\Delta = E$, as in Example 5.2.7. Show that Theorem 5.2.8 fails for the lc pair $(X, E)$: find a morphism $f \colon X \to Z$ and a Cartier divisor $N$ with $N - (K_X + E) \sim f^* A$ such that $R^1 f_* \mathcal{O}_X(N)$ has torsion, using $f = \mu$ and $N = K_X + E + \mu^* A$ for a suitable $A$.
