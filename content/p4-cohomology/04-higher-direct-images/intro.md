# Higher direct images

The cohomology of a sheaf on a scheme $X$ over a base $Y$ varies with the point of $Y$, and the higher direct images $R^q f_* \mathcal{F}$ of P2 Ch. 7 are the sheaves on $Y$ that package this variation. This chapter proves that for a quasi-compact separated morphism and a quasi-coherent sheaf they are quasi-coherent and are computed over an affine open $V \subseteq Y$ by the cohomology of the preimage of $V$, so that the results of Chapters 1 to 3 over an affine base become statements about sheaves on $Y$.

Section 4.1 proves the quasi-coherence and the affine description, the vanishing of higher direct images along affine morphisms, and the invariance of cohomology under affine morphisms with no hypothesis on the target. Section 4.2 proves that the higher direct images of a coherent sheaf along a projective morphism to a Noetherian scheme are coherent, the relative form of Serre vanishing and global generation, and the projection formula for locally free sheaves. Section 4.3 combines these with the Leray spectral sequence of P2 Ch. 4. It computes the higher direct images of the sheaves $\mathcal{O}(n)$ on a projective bundle, shows that the cohomology of the structure sheaf does not change under a projective bundle, and computes the higher direct images of the sheaves $\mathcal{O}(mE)$ along the blow-up of a point of the plane.

In the minimal model program a contraction is a proper morphism $f \colon X \to Z$ with $f_* \mathcal{O}_X = \mathcal{O}_Z$, and the higher direct images $R^q f_* \mathcal{O}_X$ measure how far the cohomology of $X$ differs from that of $Z$. A variety has rational singularities when $R^q f_* \mathcal{O}_Y = 0$ for $q > 0$ along a resolution $f \colon Y \to X$, and the relative Kawamata-Viehweg vanishing theorem is a statement about higher direct images. The blow-up computation of Section 4.3 is the simplest instance of both: the blow-up of a smooth point has $R^1 \pi_* \mathcal{O} = 0$, and $R^1 \pi_* \mathcal{O}(mE)$ becomes nonzero as soon as $mE - K$ stops being relatively nef.

The chapter follows Hartshorne, Section III.8, Vakil, Chapter 18, and Görtz-Wedhorn II, with details from the Stacks project. It uses Chapters 1 and 2, the derived functors and spectral sequences of P2 Ch. 3, Ch. 4 and Ch. 7, the projective bundles of P3 Ch. 6 and the blow-ups of P3 Ch. 11.
