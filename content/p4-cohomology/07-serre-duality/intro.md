# Serre duality

On a smooth projective variety $X$ of dimension $n$ over a field $k$, the cohomology of a vector bundle $\mathcal{E}$ in degree $i$ is dual to the cohomology of $\mathcal{E}^\vee \otimes \omega_X$ in degree $n - i$, where $\omega_X = \bigwedge^n \Omega_X$ is the canonical sheaf. This is Serre duality. It is the reason the canonical sheaf is at the centre of the minimal model program: Kodaira vanishing and its generalizations in Volume 5 are statements about $H^i(X, \omega_X \otimes L)$, the Riemann-Roch theorem on a surface pairs $D$ with $K_X - D$, and the numerical behaviour of $K_X$ on curves, which drives the cone theorem, is computed through duality on those curves. This chapter proves Serre duality on projective schemes over a field, in the form of Hartshorne, Chapter III.7.

Section 7.1 introduces the Ext groups $\operatorname{Ext}^i_X(\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G})$ and the Ext sheaves $\mathcal{E}xt^i_X(\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G})$ of $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules, computes them for locally free sheaves and by locally free resolutions, and compares the two on a projective scheme after a high twist. Section 7.2 proves Serre duality on projective space, with dualizing sheaf $\omega_{\mathbb{P}^n} = \mathcal{O}(-n-1)$, from the computation of the cohomology of $\mathcal{O}(m)$ in Chapter 2 and the universality of derived functors. Section 7.3 defines the dualizing sheaf $\omega^\circ_X$ of a projective scheme, constructs it as $\mathcal{E}xt^r_P(\mathcal{O}_X, \omega_P)$ for an embedding $X \subseteq P = \mathbb{P}^N$ of codimension $r$, proves that full duality in all degrees holds exactly when $X$ is Cohen-Macaulay, and identifies $\omega^\circ_X$ with $\omega_P \otimes \det \mathcal{N}_{X/P}$ for a local complete intersection, hence with the canonical sheaf $\omega_X$ when $X$ is smooth.

In the minimal model program, the varieties are normal and usually singular, and the canonical sheaf of a normal variety is defined in Section P3.12 as the pushforward of the canonical sheaf of the smooth locus. Section 7.3 shows that this agrees with the dualizing sheaf $\omega^\circ_X$ of this chapter for a normal projective variety, by the depth two property of $\omega^\circ_X$, and that full Serre duality holds on a Cohen-Macaulay variety, the class that includes all varieties with rational singularities (Chapter 9). Chapter 8 develops the local side of duality, and Chapter 9 places this chapter inside Grothendieck duality.

The sources are Hartshorne III.6 and III.7, Vakil Chapter 30, Görtz-Wedhorn II, and the Stacks project chapter on duality for schemes. Section P2.8.4 stated the derived category form of the same theorems, and the reader can compare the two.
