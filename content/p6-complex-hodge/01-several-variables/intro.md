# Several complex variables

This chapter develops the local theory of holomorphic functions of several complex variables that the rest of the volume uses on charts of complex manifolds. Three phenomena separate it from the theory of one variable. A holomorphic function of two or more variables extends across compact holes (Hartogs), so its zeros and singularities are never isolated. Its ring of germs $\mathcal{O}_n$ is no longer a discrete valuation ring, but the Weierstrass theorems reduce it to polynomials over $\mathcal{O}_{n-1}$, and from that reduction it is a Noetherian factorial regular local ring of dimension $n$. The inhomogeneous Cauchy-Riemann equations $\bar\partial \beta = \alpha$ are overdetermined, with the compatibility condition $\bar\partial \alpha = 0$, and the Dolbeault-Grothendieck lemma solves them on products of discs and annuli.

Section 1.1 proves the Cauchy integral formula on polydiscs and its consequences: power series expansions, the Cauchy estimates, the identity theorem and the maximum principle, the inverse and implicit function theorems, and the solution of $\partial u/\partial \bar z = g$ in one variable by the Cauchy kernel. Section 1.2 solves the $\bar\partial$-equation with compact support when $n \geq 2$ and deduces the Hartogs extension theorem, then proves the Riemann extension theorem across the zero set of a holomorphic function. Section 1.3 proves the Weierstrass division and preparation theorems and deduces that $\mathcal{O}_n$ is Noetherian, factorial, normal and regular. Section 1.4 introduces forms of type $(p,q)$ and the operators $\partial$ and $\bar\partial$, proves the Dolbeault-Grothendieck lemma and the local $\partial\bar\partial$-lemma, and shows on $\mathbb{C}^2 \setminus \{0\}$ that the lemma fails on general open sets.

Chapter 2 globalizes these results. The Dolbeault-Grothendieck lemma becomes the statement that the Dolbeault complex is a fine resolution of $\Omega^p_X$, so that Dolbeault cohomology computes $H^q(X, \Omega^p_X)$, and the Hartogs theorem computes the sections of the line bundles $\mathcal{O}(d)$ on projective space. The local $\partial\bar\partial$-lemma gives the local potentials of Kähler forms in Chapter 4.

The chapter follows Huybrechts, Section 1.1 (Huy05), Voisin, Chapter 1 (Voi02), Griffiths and Harris, Section 0.1 (GH78), and Hörmander, Chapters II and VI (Hor90).
