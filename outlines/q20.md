# Q-20: the new page estimates of Volumes 1, 5 and 6

Question Q-20 of spec 16 asks how P7 and P8 share their material with Volumes 1, 5 and 6. The answer is (a), the rule of spec 11.4:
- the P volume gives the absolute case and the smooth case, with full proofs;
- the program volume gives the relative case, the singular case and the case of pairs, and it cites the P volume for the rest.

The page estimates of spec 11.2 for Volumes 1, 5 and 6 come from report F. Report F did not know the P volumes, so those estimates are upper bounds. This file measures the three volumes again from the outlines `p7.md` and `p8.md`, as milestone S2 asks (spec 15).

The method has two steps:
- Take each main theorem and each worked example of spec 11.2.1 for the volume.
- If the outline of P7 or P8 proves it in the absolute or smooth case, the volume cites that proof and proves only the relative, singular or log form. Otherwise the volume proves it.

The chapters below are a plan, not a contents list. The writer of each volume can change them, and a change to a page count goes into this file and into spec 11.2.

## Volume 5: Vanishing theorems and multiplier ideals

The old estimate is 340 pages and the new one is 210. The volume cites P7 Ch. 8 to 11:
- Kodaira and Kawamata-Viehweg vanishing for a smooth variety with an snc boundary;
- Grauert-Riemenschneider vanishing;
- Kollár's injectivity and torsion-freeness for a smooth variety;
- multiplier ideals on a smooth variety, with Nadel vanishing, restriction, subadditivity and Skoda;
- Howald's theorem;
- the failure of vanishing in characteristic $p$.

The example of Howald's theorem moves to P7 Ch. 10. The volume computes it again for a pair.

## Volume 6: Rational curves and bend-and-break

The old estimate is 300 pages and the new one is 200. The volume cites P8 Ch. 1 to 3:
- Hom schemes and their dimension bound;
- the two forms of bend-and-break, with the degree bound in characteristic $p$;
- the definitions of uniruled and rationally connected varieties, and the free and very free curves.

The example of rational curves on $\mathbb{P}^n$ moves to P8 Ch. 1. The volume keeps the example as an exercise.
