# Outline of P8: Tools of birational geometry

This is the outline of P8 for milestone S2 (spec 15) and question Q-20 (spec 16). It lists the sections of each chapter, the page estimate of each chapter, and the place where the program volumes use the chapter. The writer of P8 starts from it. A change to the outline is a pull request to this file.

The spine texts are Debarre, *Higher-dimensional algebraic geometry* (Deb01), Kollár-Mori, Ch. 1 and 4 (KM98), Kollár, *Lectures on resolution of singularities* (Kol07), Cox, Little and Schenck, *Toric varieties*, and Reid, *Young person's guide to canonical singularities* (Rei87). The volume needs P5, P7 Ch. 1 to 5 for Parts A to C, and P4 Ch. 9 and P7 Ch. 8 for Part D (spec 11.1).

The rule of spec 11.4 fixes the scope. P8 proves the absolute and smooth results: bend-and-break on a smooth projective variety, resolution of curves and surfaces, toric geometry up to the toric cone, and the local theory of the classical surface singularities. Volume 6 proves Mori's theorems with bend-and-break as a tool, Volume 3 runs the toric program, and Volume 4 treats the singularities of pairs.

## The sections

### 1. Hom and Hilbert schemes (45 pages)

1. The Hilbert functor (P3 Ch. 13) and the existence of Hilbert schemes, with the proof in outline.
2. The scheme $\mathrm{Hom}(C, X)$ as an open subscheme of a Hilbert scheme.
3. The tangent space $H^0(C, f^*T_X)$ and the dimension bound $\dim_{[f]} \mathrm{Hom}(C, X) \ge -K_X \cdot f_*C + n\,\chi(\mathcal{O}_C)$.
4. Morphisms with fixed points; the bound with $n$ points fixed.
5. Examples: lines on $\mathbb{P}^n$ and on a cubic surface; $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb{P}^1, \mathbb{P}^n)$.

### 2. Bend-and-break (35 pages)

1. Mori's first bend-and-break: a curve that moves with a point fixed breaks off a rational curve.
2. The second form: a rational curve that moves with two points fixed breaks.
3. The degree bound: a rational curve with $-K_X \cdot C \le n + 1$ through a point, given a $K_X$-negative curve, on a smooth projective variety in characteristic $p$.
4. The reduction mod $p$ from characteristic $0$, in outline.

Volume 6 proves Mori's theorem on smooth Fano varieties and the cone theorem for smooth varieties from these lemmas (spec 11.4).

### 3. Uniruled and rationally connected varieties (40 pages)

1. Uniruled varieties; free rational curves; in characteristic 0, uniruled if and only if there is a free rational curve.
2. Rationally connected and rationally chain connected varieties; very free curves.
3. The negative Kodaira dimension of uniruled varieties.
4. Smoothing of combs, in outline.
5. Examples: $\mathbb{P}^n$, Fano hypersurfaces of low degree, conic bundles.

The rational connectedness of Fano manifolds (Kollár, Miyaoka, Mori) and the theorem of Graber, Harris and Starr are Vol. 6.

### 4. Resolution of curves and surfaces (40 pages)

1. Normalization of curves; resolution of curves.
2. Embedded resolution of plane curves by blow-ups of points; the cusp and the tacnode.
3. Resolution of surfaces: Zariski's and Lipman's routes, the proof for surfaces with the method of Hirzebruch-Jung for quotient points.
4. The minimal resolution of a normal surface and its uniqueness.

### 5. Hironaka's theorem, as statements (25 pages)

1. Resolution of singularities and of pairs: log resolutions with snc support.
2. Principalization, and the functorial resolution (Bierstone-Milman, Włodarczyk, Kollár), as statements.
3. Weak factorization (Abramovich, Karu, Matsuki, Włodarczyk), as a statement.
4. What is known in characteristic $p$: curves, surfaces, 3-folds for $p > 5$ (Cossart-Piltant), as statements.

### 6. Cones, fans and toric varieties (45 pages)

1. Lattices, rational polyhedral cones and duality; Gordan's lemma.
2. Affine toric varieties from cones; fans and gluing.
3. The orbit-cone correspondence.
4. Completeness, smoothness and simpliciality read off the fan.
5. Examples: $\mathbb{P}^n$, $\mathbb{F}_n$, weighted projective spaces, $\frac1r(1, a)$.

### 7. Toric divisors (40 pages)

1. Torus-invariant divisors and $\mathrm{Cl}(X_\Sigma)$; the exact sequence $M \to \mathbb{Z}^{\Sigma(1)} \to \mathrm{Cl}(X_\Sigma) \to 0$.
2. Cartier and $\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier toric divisors; support functions; $\mathbb{Q}$-factorial if and only if simplicial.
3. The canonical divisor $K_X = -\sum D_\rho$.
4. Global sections and polytopes; ample and nef toric divisors by convexity.

### 8. Toric resolution and the toric cone (35 pages)

1. Subdivisions of fans and toric resolution of singularities.
2. Torus-invariant curves and their intersection numbers with divisors, from the wall relation.
3. The toric cone theorem: $\overline{NE}(X_\Sigma)$ is spanned by the torus-invariant curves; nef is positive on walls.
4. The Atiyah flop as two triangulations of a square.

Volume 3 proves the toric cone theorem with the program in view, and the Lean proof is `MinimalModel.Toric.toricConeTheorem`. P8 states the theorem and proves it for smooth complete toric surfaces, and cites Volume 3 for the general case. This keeps one full proof, in the program volume, as rule (a) of Q-20 asks for a result that the program needs in a sharper form.

### 9. Quotient singularities (35 pages)

1. Finite group actions and quotients $\mathbb{C}^n / G$.
2. Cyclic quotient singularities $\frac1r(a_1, \dots, a_n)$ as toric varieties.
3. Pseudo-reflections and the theorem of Chevalley-Shephard-Todd.
4. Hirzebruch-Jung continued fractions and the resolution of $\frac1r(1, a)$.

### 10. Du Val singularities (40 pages)

1. The ADE list of equations.
2. Resolution graphs and the Dynkin diagrams.
3. Du Val singularities as quotients by finite subgroups of $SL_2(\mathbb{C})$.
4. Characterizations: canonical, rational double points, Gorenstein rational; the proofs on surfaces.

### 11. Rational singularities (35 pages)

1. The definition by $R^i f_* \mathcal{O}_Y = 0$; independence of the resolution.
2. Rational surface singularities (Artin): the fundamental cycle and the multiplicity.
3. Quotient singularities are rational; Cohen-Macaulay property of rational singularities (P4 Ch. 9).
4. Elkik's theorem and Kempf's criterion, as statements.

### 12. Cones over varieties (40 pages)

1. The affine cone $C(V, L)$ over a projective variety with an ample line bundle.
2. When the cone is normal, $\mathbb{Q}$-Gorenstein, Cohen-Macaulay, rational.
3. The cone over a rational normal curve is $\frac1n(1, 1)$.
4. The cone over an elliptic curve, and over a curve of genus $g$.

### 13. First discrepancies (45 pages)

1. The canonical divisor of a normal variety (P3 Ch. 12) and $\mathbb{Q}$-Gorenstein varieties.
2. Discrepancies of exceptional divisors on one resolution; the formula for the blow-up of a smooth point.
3. Terminal and canonical singularities on surfaces; the discrepancies of $A_1$, $\frac1n(1,1)$ and of cones over curves.
4. The preview of Volume 4: klt and lc, pairs, independence of the model.
