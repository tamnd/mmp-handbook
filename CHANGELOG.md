## v0.0.4 - 2026-11-26

### Added

- Volume P3, Chapter 7, Dimension, smoothness and normalization: dimension and codimension with the transcendence degree theorem and fibre dimensions, the Zariski tangent space and regular schemes, smoothness over a field by the Jacobian criterion with the open smooth locus, and normal schemes with Serre's criterion, the Hartogs property, finite morphisms and the normalization.
- Volume P3, Chapter 6, Proj, O(n) and P(E): graded rings and Proj, the sheaves O(n) and graded modules, morphisms to projective space, ample and very ample sheaves with Serre's theorem, and relative Proj with the projective bundle P(E) and the Hirzebruch surfaces.
- Volume 3, Chapter 4, the toric cone theorem: invariant curves and the wall relation with the intersection formula on a simplicial wall, support functions and the combinatorial cone theorem with the generic segment proof, linked to node MM6EHAYFKV, and the cone of curves with the nef criterion, $N_1$ as the space of relations and the weighted blow-up of $\mathbb{P}^3$ with weights $(1, 1, 2)$.
- Volume P3, Chapter 5, Quasi-coherent and coherent sheaves: the sheaf of a module and its adjunction, the equivalence with modules on an affine scheme, the abelian categories of quasi-coherent and coherent sheaves, ideal sheaves, Nakayama's lemma for sheaves, pullback and pushforward, the relative spectrum and total spaces, with 28 exercises.
- Volume P3, Chapter 4, separated and proper morphisms: the diagonal and the criterion with affine open sets, the doubled origin and the separatedness of projective space, stability of separated morphisms, closed equalizers and graphs, the reduced-to-separated theorem, universally closed and proper morphisms with finite morphisms proper, constant global functions on proper reduced connected schemes, the properness of projective space by elimination theory, and the valuative criteria.
- Volume P3, Chapter 3, morphisms of schemes: morphisms over a base and the affineness criterion, closed immersions with the closed subschemes of an affine scheme and the reduced induced structure, fibre products by gluing with the affine case, fibres of a morphism with the families xy = t and t = x^2, and quasi-compact, affine, finite and finite type morphisms with their stability under composition and base change.
- Volume P3, Chapter 2, schemes: schemes and open subschemes, gluing of schemes and of morphisms, the affine line with a doubled origin, projective space over a ring by gluing with its global functions and its points over local rings, reduced, integral and Noetherian schemes with the affine communication lemma, generic points and function fields, varieties, and the functor of points with the theorem that points with values in rings determine a scheme.
- Volume P3, schemes, divisors and line bundles: the volume file with the plan of 13 chapters, the volume intro, and Chapter 1, affine schemes: the structure sheaf, stalks and basic open sets, morphisms into affine schemes and the anti-equivalence with rings, closed subschemes and the first examples, with the books GW20, EGA1 and Mum99.
- Volume P2, Chapter 8, derived categories: the homotopy category, cones and triangles, the derived category with roofs, injective replacement and Ext, total derived functors with $R\Gamma$, $Rf_*$, $Lf^*$, $\otimes^L$ and $R\mathcal{H}om$, the projection formula, Grothendieck duality, dualizing complexes and the canonical sheaf, and Kempf's criterion, with the books Huy06, Spa88, Lip09, Ver96, Kov00, Con00 and GR70.

### Added

- Volume P1, commutative algebra for geometry: the volume file, the books Mat86 and Eis95, and Chapters 1 and 2 (rings, modules and flatness; localization, the prime spectrum and primary decomposition).
- Volume 17, open problems: the first dated report, of 2026-11-26, in seven chapters (termination; abundance and non-vanishing; ACC and LSC for mlds; boundedness; characteristic $p$; foliations and Kähler; how the project tracks the problems).
- Volume 3, Chapters 1 to 3: cones and fans, toric varieties from fans, toric divisors and the canonical divisor.
- Volume P1, Chapters 3 and 4: Noetherian and Artinian rings (chain conditions, the Hilbert basis theorem, Noetherian spaces, length and Artinian rings), and integral extensions (integral closure, going up and going down, normal domains, Noether normalization and the Jacobson property).
- Volume P1, Chapter 5: dimension theory (Krull dimension and height, Krull's principal ideal and height theorems, Hilbert-Samuel functions and the dimension theorem, the dimension of affine domains and of fibres).
- Volume P1, Chapter 6: completion (inverse limits, adic completion and the Krull intersection theorem, completion of Noetherian rings and its flatness, Hensel's lemma and the Cohen structure theorem).
- Volume P1, Chapter 7: DVRs, Dedekind domains and valuations (valuation rings and the existence theorem, the characterization of discrete valuation rings and the order of vanishing as a length, unique factorization of ideals and the class group).
- Volume P1, Chapter 8: normal rings and class groups (Krull's theorem and the algebraic Hartogs theorem, Weil divisors, Nagata's localization sequence and the class group of a polynomial ring, the class groups of the cones xy = z^n and xw = yz, Cartier divisors, the Picard group and Q-factoriality).
- Volume P1, Chapter 9: regular local rings (the associated graded ring of a regular local ring, regular local rings are domains, quotients by parameters, completion, the Jacobian criterion and the singular locus, minimal resolutions and the Koszul complex, Serre's characterization and localization, the theorem of Auslander and Buchsbaum, smooth varieties are locally factorial).
- Volume P1, Chapter 10: depth, Cohen-Macaulay and Gorenstein rings (regular sequences, Rees's theorem and depth, depth and completion, the Auslander-Buchsbaum formula, Cohen-Macaulay local rings and complete intersections, Cohen-Macaulay as freeness over a regular subring, the type, Artinian Gorenstein rings, Veronese rings and the cone over the twisted cubic).
- Volume P1, Chapter 11: Serre's criterion and reflexive modules (Serre's conditions R_k and S_k, depth two and the extension of homomorphisms, the algebraic Hartogs property, Serre's criteria for reducedness and normality, normal surfaces are Cohen-Macaulay, normality of hypersurfaces, reflexive modules over normal domains, divisorial ideals and the class group, reflexive powers).
- Volume P1, Chapter 12: Kähler differentials (derivations and the universal property, the first fundamental and conormal sequences, presentations by the Jacobian matrix, differentials of field extensions and separability, regularity as freeness of the differentials over a perfect field, the canonical module and canonical divisor of a normal affine domain, residue forms).
- Volume P1, Chapter 13: graded rings (homogeneous ideals and primes, associated primes of graded modules, graded Nakayama, Hilbert series and dimension, graded Noether normalization, graded regular rings are polynomial rings, Proj and its affine charts, Veronese subrings, the projective Nullstellensatz, weighted projective spaces, blow-ups and section rings).
