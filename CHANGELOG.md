## v0.0.2 - 2026-11-26

### Fixed

- Volume 1, Section 1.5: the ACC conjecture for minimal log discrepancies is open from dimension 3, not from dimension 4. The lower semicontinuity is open from dimension 4.

### Added

- Volume P1, commutative algebra for geometry: the volume file, the books Mat86 and Eis95, and Chapters 1 and 2 (rings, modules and flatness; localization, the prime spectrum and primary decomposition).
- Volume 17, open problems: the first dated report, of 2026-11-26, in seven chapters (termination; abundance and non-vanishing; ACC and LSC for mlds; boundedness; characteristic $p$; foliations and Kähler; how the project tracks the problems).
- Volume 3, Chapters 1 to 3: cones and fans, toric varieties from fans, toric divisors and the canonical divisor.
- Volume P1, Chapters 3 and 4: Noetherian and Artinian rings (chain conditions, the Hilbert basis theorem, Noetherian spaces, length and Artinian rings), and integral extensions (integral closure, going up and going down, normal domains, Noether normalization and the Jacobson property).
- Volume P1, Chapter 5: dimension theory (Krull dimension and height, Krull's principal ideal and height theorems, Hilbert-Samuel functions and the dimension theorem, the dimension of affine domains and of fibres).
- Volume P1, Chapter 6: completion (inverse limits, adic completion and the Krull intersection theorem, completion of Noetherian rings and its flatness, Hensel's lemma and the Cohen structure theorem).
- Volume P1, Chapter 7: DVRs, Dedekind domains and valuations (valuation rings and the existence theorem, the characterization of discrete valuation rings and the order of vanishing as a length, unique factorization of ideals and the class group).
- Volume P1, Chapter 8: normal rings and class groups (Krull's theorem and the algebraic Hartogs theorem, Weil divisors, Nagata's localization sequence and the class group of a polynomial ring, the class groups of the cones xy = z^n and xw = yz, Cartier divisors, the Picard group and Q-factoriality).
- Volume P1, Chapter 9: regular local rings (the associated graded ring of a regular local ring, regular local rings are domains, quotients by parameters, completion, the Jacobian criterion and the singular locus, minimal resolutions and the Koszul complex, Serre's characterization and localization, the theorem of Auslander and Buchsbaum, smooth varieties are locally factorial).
- Volume P1, Chapter 10: depth, Cohen-Macaulay and Gorenstein rings (regular sequences, Rees's theorem and depth, depth and completion, the Auslander-Buchsbaum formula, Cohen-Macaulay local rings and complete intersections, Cohen-Macaulay as freeness over a regular subring, the type, Artinian Gorenstein rings, Veronese rings and the cone over the twisted cubic).
